## Rigor conventions used here
* Modular rank is a lower bound for the rational rank.  A fixed-D exclusion is called SEALED when the modular nullity is
  matched by exactly verified rational null vectors (so rank_Q = rank_p) and the augmented modular rank exceeds it; or when
  the exact fmpz/fmpq rank of the augmented system exceeds that of the system.
* E1 kernel dimensions: upper bounds over Q from any prime p for which dim J(F)_n mod p equals the complete-intersection
  Hilbert function in the degrees used (then rank_p J = rank_Q J); lower bounds from the G-graded Hilbert function
  (dimension-forced) or from exactly verified elements.
* Relaxation m-primary tests: the relaxation ideal is homogeneous, G -> G + hE invariant, generated by reductions of rational
  quadrics (annihilator of J_{2d} reduces correctly because dim J_{2d} mod p is the c.i. value); on the gauge slice an
  m-primary modular ideal forces V(I_Q) = {0}: a nonzero projective zero over Q-bar would reduce, after scaling to a
  primitive p-integral vector, to a nonzero zero of the reduced ideal mod a prime above p.  (This argument needs
  homogeneity; per-form Rabinowitsch tests I + (l - 1) = (1) mod p are NOT transferable and are labelled modular evidence.)
* Everything labelled "screened" or "modular evidence" is not a proof.
