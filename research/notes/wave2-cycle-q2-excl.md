## Transfer principles used (stated once)
Let p be a prime with `F mod p` smooth (`vdim J = 15625`; checked at 2^31−1 and 10⁹+7). Then `∂_iF mod p` is a regular sequence, so every Jacobian matrix `J_k` has `rank_p = rank_ℚ` and its cokernel over `ℤ_(p)` is torsion-free. Consequences: (T1) a zero E1-annihilator mod p is zero over ℚ (`dim Ann_ℚ ≤ dim Ann_p`); linear independence of classes modulo `J` mod p implies independence over ℚ. (T2) If a denominator `D` over a number field admits a certificate, then (after scaling `D` to be 𝔭-integral and primitive) `A·D ∈ J` and `H(D) ∈ J` hold over the valuation ring, hence for the reduction `D̄ ≠ 0` over `F_𝔭`; so the mod-p relaxation variety of a family contains the reduction of every admissible member — emptiness over `F̄_p` excludes the family over ℚ̄ (and ℂ, the problem being defined over ℚ). (T3) Fixed-denominator inconsistency transfers from mod p to ℚ only when `rank_p M = rank_ℚ M`; we never rely on this and instead give exact rational Farkas vectors. (T4) Polynomial Farkas vectors `y(a)` with `y(a)ᵀM(a) ≡ 0` over ℚ exclude every `a` with `y(a)ᵀb(a) ≠ 0`, without any reduction.

A bare modular inconsistency transfers to char 0 only if `rank_p(M) = rank_Q(M)`, which one prime does not certify (the "bad prime" caveat applies to every modular exclusion in the thread/dossier); the Farkas vector removes it.
