### 1.3 Gauge invariance (proved; used in every search)
If (D, N, Q) is a certificate normalized by N(F) = A·D (always possible), then for every h ∈ S_{d-6}
(D + hF, N + hA E/6, Q + h div N - N(h) + h^2 A) is a certificate.  Denominators are therefore searched modulo F·S_{d-6}.
