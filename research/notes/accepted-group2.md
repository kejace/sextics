## 1. family-block-cube  ("Block cube · θ = 0")

**(F, q, A).** `F = X0^6 + X0^4 Y0^2 + 2 X0^2 Y0^4 + 3 Y0^6 + (X1^6 + X1^3 X2^3 + 2 X2^6) + Y1^6 + Y2^6`
`= p(u,w) + B(X1,X2) + Y1^6 + Y2^6` with the active binary cubic `p(u,w) = u^3 + u^2 w + 2 u w^2 + 3 w^3`,
`q = 1`, `A = X0^3 Y0^3`.

**Symmetry [verified].** Sign group `(Z/2)^5` (order 32): `X0, Y0, Y1, Y2` individually, `(X1,X2)` jointly.
Coordinate permutations: only `Y1 ↔ Y2`. `D` is invariant under all sign changes but **not** under
`Y1 ↔ Y2` (coefficients 1 vs 1681/1701) — the certificate breaks that symmetry deliberately (see mechanism).

**Method.** Constructive, strict, `d = 6`, `Q = 0`. Osculating-cube ("block-cube family") construction at
family parameter θ = 0.

**Denominator [verified].** `D = (2 X0^2 + 9 Y0^2)^3 / 243 + Y1^6 + (1681/1701) Y2^6` (6 terms; lies in
`Q[u, w, Y1^6, Y2^6]`; irreducible over Q by sympy `factor_list`).

**Numerators [record, verified].** degree 7; `N1 = 0` (`X1` is never moved); every `N_i` is divisible by
`X0·Y0` (the Jacobian density of `(X0,Y0) ↦ (u,w)`); `N5 = (1681/10206)·A·Y2`, `N4 = Y1·(…)`,
`N2 = X2·X0^3 Y0·(…)`, `N0 = Y0·(…)`, `N3 = X0·(…)`. In the target coordinates `(u,w,X1,X2,Y1,Y2)` the
field is `X0 Y0 · W / D` with polynomial components
`W_{Y2} = (1681/10206) u w Y2`, `W_{X2} = −(82/5103) u (5u+2w) X2`, `W_{Y1} = (5u+2w)(8u+405w) Y1 / 10206`,
and cubic `W_u, W_w` in `(u,w)` plus terms in `X1^3 X2^3, X2^6, Y1^6`.

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* The cube `C = (2u+9w)^3/243` osculates `p` at θ = u/w = 0: `p − C = 5 u^2 (47u + 27w)/243` has a double
  root at `u = 0` (p and C agree with their first derivative at `(u:w) = (0:1)`) [verified].
* The pencil `p − λC` has discriminant (in `u`, `w = 1`) `−25 (λ−1)(1681λ − 1701)/243` [verified]: it is
  singular exactly at `λ₁ = 1` (double contact), `λ₂ = 1701/1681`, and `λ = ∞` (the cube itself, triple root).
* The three spectator channels are attached at exactly these three parameters: `Y1^6` enters `D` with
  coefficient `1 = 1/λ₁`, `Y2^6` with `1681/1701 = 1/λ₂`, the `(X1,X2)` block with `0` (λ = ∞). This is the
  precise content of "the unusual last coefficient is forced by the pencil compatibility"; replacing
  1681/1701 by 1 would put both `Y` spectators at the same pencil member [verified for the discriminant;
  the necessity claim is the record's].
* Once `D` is fixed, `N` was found by linear algebra on degree-7 character supports (`N(F)=A·D` and
  `D·div N = N(D)` both imposed) [record].
* `div N = (1681/1701)·A`, so `v(F − D) = (20/1701)·A ≠ 0`: the field does **not** annihilate the complement
  `F − D = (235/243)X0^6 + (5/9)X0^4 Y0^2 + B(X1,X2) + (20/1701)Y2^6` [verified]. Hence this is not an
  instance of the spectral §2.1 constructor (`W/(uw)` is not polynomial); it reads as a sum of channel
  fields with a common denominator. I did **not** reconstruct that decomposition.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2744 B; `Witness.lean` 22025 B / 357 lines; coprimality via `z = X1`
(`F ∋ X1^6`, lc 1), `fFiber = z^6 + z^3 + 11`, `dFiber = 1411/189`.

**Related.** Same mechanism family: `f3-block-x0-quartic-strict` (Hesse `t = 1`, contact conic, `d = 4`),
`family-hesse-web`, `family-sylvester-norm`, `family-root-cube` (all "binary cubic in squares"). Open
instances: none on this variety; the only open instance with a binary-block decomposition is
`four-plus-two-residual` (`F = H(X0,X1,Y0,Y1) + B(X2,Y2)`, target `X0^3 Y0^3`), whose `why.md` states that
the Chapter 2a binary-block and Euler mechanisms do not apply.

**[mine] Conjecture / next step.** For general θ, let `C_θ` be the cube osculating `p` at `u/w = θ`, let
`λ₂(θ)` be the second finite root of `disc(p − λ C_θ)`, and set `D_θ = C_θ + Y1^6 + Y2^6/λ₂(θ)`. The
record's wording ("what happens when special pencil members collide") suggests the family degenerates where
`λ₂(θ) = 1`. Unverified.

## 2. family-hesse-web  ("Hesse web · t = −2")

**(F, q, A).** `F = X0^6 − 2 X0^2 X1^2 Y0^2 + X1^6 + 2 X2^6 + Y0^6 + 3 Y1^6 + 5 Y2^6`
`= E_t(u, w, x) + 2X2^6 + 3Y1^6 + 5Y2^6` with `x = X1^2`, `E_t = u^3 + w^3 + x^3 + t·u w x`, `t = −2`;
`q = 1`, `A = X0^3 Y0^3`.

**Symmetry [verified].** Fully coordinatewise even (sign group order 64). Permutations: the full `S_3` on
`{X0, X1, Y0}` (E_t is symmetric in u,w,x; the spectator coefficients 2,3,5 are distinct so nothing else).
`D` is invariant under all sign changes and under `X0 ↔ Y0`, not under `X0 ↔ X1`.

**Method.** Constructive, strict, `d = 6`, `Q = 0`. Three tangent-line ("contact") channels from the flex
`O = (−1:1:0)` of the Hesse cubic, with a **norm denominator** over a cubic field.

**Denominator [verified].** With `a = u + w = X0^2 + Y0^2`:
`D = a^3 + t a^2 x + 4 x^3 = a^3 − 2 a^2 x + 4 x^3 = ∏_{i=1}^{3} (a + C_i x) = Norm_{Q(C)/Q}(a + C x)`,
where `C^3 − t C^2 − 4 = C^3 + 2C^2 − 4 = 0` (irreducible over Q; `e₁ = −t = 2, e₂ = 0, e₃ = 4`).
`D` is irreducible over Q (8 terms) and splits into three conjugate quadrics `X0^2 + Y0^2 + C_i X1^2` over
the cubic field.

**Numerators [record, verified].** degree 7; only `N0, N1, N3` nonzero (`X2, Y1, Y2` untouched, so the
spectator coefficients 2,3,5 never matter):
`N0 = Y0(2X0^4X1^2 + 3X0^4Y0^2 + 2X0^2X1^2Y0^2 + 6X0^2Y0^4 − 6X1^6 + 3Y0^6)/18`,
`N1 = X0 X1 Y0 (X0^2+Y0^2)(3X0^2 − 2X1^2 + 3Y0^2)/9`,
`N3 = X0(3X0^6 + 6X0^4Y0^2 + 2X0^2X1^2Y0^2 + 3X0^2Y0^4 − 6X1^6 + 2X1^2Y0^4)/18`.
In `(u,w,x)` coordinates the field is `X0Y0·W/D` with `W_u = (3u^2w + 2u^2x + 6uw^2 + 2uwx + 3w^3 − 6x^3)/9`,
`W_w = (3u^3 + 6u^2w + 3uw^2 + 2uwx + 2w^2x − 6x^3)/9`, `W_x = 2a(3a − 2x)/9`. `div N = X0 Y0 a^2` [verified].

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* `O = (−1:1:0)` is a flex of `E_t` (the line `x = 0` meets `E_t` in `u^3 + w^3 = 0`). Lines through `O`
  are `a + C x = 0`. Restricting `E_{−2}` to such a line gives `−x·[(3C−2)w^2 + (3C^2−2C)wx + (C^3−1)x^2]`;
  the factor `x` is `O` itself and the residual quadratic has discriminant `−(3C−2)(C^3 + 2C^2 − 4)` [verified].
* Hence the tangent lines from `O` are the flex tangent `C = −t/3 = 2/3` (restriction `19x^3/27`, triple
  contact at `O`; the "fourth, flex channel", given weight 0 in the solution) and the three ordinary tangents
  with `C^3 + 2C^2 − 4 = 0`. Their tangency points are `(u:w:x) = (1:1:−κ)` with `κ = 2/C`, `κ^3 + tκ = 2`
  [verified] — the "contact conic parameter" of the record.
* Each ordinary tangent gives a logarithmic field with pole along `L_C = a + C x` (a contact of order two:
  the restriction of the cubic is a square up to the factor `x`); the three are combined with weights in
  `Q(C)` chosen so that the derivatives sum to the density of `A = X0Y0·uw` and the divergences cancel,
  then traced to Q. The common denominator is the norm `D` [record; the partial-fraction decomposition
  over `Q(C)` was not re-derived here].
* `v(F − D) = X0Y0(uw − a^2) ≠ 0`: the field does not annihilate the complement.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2722 B; `Witness.lean` 21997 B / 355 lines; coprimality via `z = X2`
(`F ∋ 2X2^6`, lc 2), `fFiber = 2z^6 + 9`, `dFiber = 4`.

**Related.** `f3-block-x0-quartic-strict` (same Hesse pencil at `t = 1` on a different `F`, `d = 4`,
two-term target; its `why.md` links this instance). Open instances: none on this variety. The Hesse-web
"web" question (which rational combinations of contact channels reach a single monomial) has no open
counterpart in the catalogue; `quadric-contact-residual` uses contact/passive-ratio language but its
obstruction is the `X0 ↔ Y0`-invariant character of `A`, which does not arise here.

## 3. family-passive-contact  ("Passive quadric · linear contact")

**(F, q, A).** `F = X0^6 + X1^6 + X0^2 X1^2 R + X2^6 + X2^2 Y1^2 Y2^2 + Y0^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6`,
`R = (13/17) Y0^2 + (5/9) Y0 Y2 + (7/11) Y2^2` (10 terms); `q = 1`, `A = X0^3 X1 X2 Y1`.

**Symmetry [verified].** Sign group order 32: `X0, X1, X2, Y1` individually, `(Y0,Y2)` jointly (the
`Y0Y2` cross term). Permutations: `X0 ↔ X1`, `X2 ↔ Y1`, and their product. `D` invariant under all signs.

**Method.** Constructive, strict, `d = 6`, `Q = 0`. "Linear contact": the singular Hesse member
(product of three lines in squared coordinates) as denominator; adjugate/logarithmic field on the active
pair, everything else passive.

**Denominator [verified].** With `u = X0^2`, `w = X1^2`, `x = −R/3`:
`D = X0^6 + X1^6 + X0^2 X1^2 R − R^3/27 = Φ(u,w,x)`, `Φ = u^3 + w^3 + x^3 − 3uwx = det circ(u,w,x)
= (u + w + x)(u^2 + w^2 + x^2 − uw − ux − wx)`.
So **`D` is reducible over Q**: `D = q₂ · q₄` with the quadric `q₂ = X0^2 + X1^2 − R/3`
(`= (5049X0^2 + 5049X1^2 − 1287Y0^2 − 935Y0Y2 − 1071Y2^2)/5049`) and a quartic `q₄` which splits over
`Q(ω)` into the two conjugate quadrics `u + ω w + ω^2 x`, `u + ω^2 w + ω x`. The pole along the rational
factor `q₂` is genuine: `N0, N1 ≢ 0 mod q₂` and `gcd(N_i, D) = 1` [verified], so the certificate does
**not** reduce to the quartic denominator used in `passive-quadratic-d4-strict-rational`.

**Numerators [verified].** degree 7; only `X0, X1` move:
`N0 = X1 X2 Y1 (X0^4/6 + X1^2 R/18)`, `N1 = X0 X2 Y1 (X0^2 X1^2/6 − R^2/54)`, `N2 = … = N5 = 0`.
`div N = A` [verified].

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* In the active squared coordinates with `x` passive, the field is
  `v = κ (Φ_u ∂_u − Φ_x ∂_w)/Φ`, `κ = X0 X1 X2 Y1 / 9` (equivalently `N0 = D v_u/(2X0)`, `N1 = D v_w/(2X1)`)
  [verified].
* The adjugate identity for the 3×3 circulant (`adj(adj M) = det(M)·M`) gives
  `Φ_u^2 − Φ_w Φ_x = 9 u Φ` [verified]; the active part of `F` is `G = u^3 + w^3 − 3uwx = Φ − x^3` with
  `x` passive, and the remaining variables are not moved, so `v(F) = v(Φ) = 9κu = X0^3 X1 X2 Y1 = A` and
  `v(D) = v(Φ) = A` as well. Hence `N(F) = N(D) = A·D` and `div N = A`: strict.
* "Linear contact": `D = G + x^3` is the member of the pencil `G + λ x^3` that degenerates to three lines
  (the singular Hesse value); the field is the logarithmic field of that reducible divisor with the passive
  density `X2 Y1`.
* The quadratic `R` enters only through `x = −R/3`; since `Y0, Y2` are never differentiated, an arbitrary
  binary quadratic (including the `Y0Y2` cross term) is tolerated — this is exactly the "passive dependence"
  the instance was designed to test. The second tie `X2^2 Y1^2 Y2^2` is passive too.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2854 B; `Witness.lean` 23568 B / 360 lines; coprimality via `z = X2`
(`F ∋ X2^6`), `fFiber = z^6 + z^2 + 11708/1683`, `dFiber = 473552900020/128711132649`.

**Related.** `passive-quadratic-d4-strict-rational` (`R = Y0^2 + Y2^2`, quartic denominator, Hamiltonian
field with conjugate residues over `Q(√−3)`, two-term target; its `why.md` names this instance as the
cross-term extension). Open instances: none on this variety. Mechanism-wise the closest open target is
`quadric-contact-residual` (quadratic coordinates `S = 2(X0^2+Y0^2)`, `T = 4X0Y0`, passive ratios), but
its obstruction (target invariant under `X0 ↔ Y0`, so not a Jacobian density of that map) has no analogue
here, where `A` is by construction the density sector of the square map.

## 4. family-root-cube  ("Root cube · crossed blocks")

**(F, q, A).** 26-term `F` (see `instance.rec`); `q = 1`, `A = X0 X1 X2^3 Y0`. Structural decomposition
[verified]: with `R = X0^2 − Y0^2`, `S = X0^2 + Y0^2`,
`P1 = X1^6 + 2X1^4Y1^2 + 3X1^2Y1^4 + 5Y1^6`, `P2 = X2^6 + 2X2^4Y2^2 + 3X2^2Y2^4 + 7Y2^6`:

```
F = C + D,
D = 3 R^3 + (101/103) [ R^2 (2X2^2 + Y2^2) + R (X2^4 + Y2^4) ] + P2(X2,Y2),
C = 2 R S^2 + (101/103) [ S^2 (X2^2 + 2Y2^2) + S (X1^3 X2 + X1^2 Y1 Y2) ] + P1(X1,Y1).
```
The active block `5u^3 − 7u^2w + 7uw^2 − 5w^3 = 3R^3 + 2RS^2` (`α = 3, κ = 2`; `R = u − w` is a root
factor of the cubic).

**Symmetry [verified].** Sign group order 16: `X0, Y0` individually; `(X1,X2)` and `(Y1,Y2)` jointly.
No coordinate permutations. `D` invariant under all signs.

**Method.** Constructive, strict, `d = 6`, `Q = 0`. Weighted derivation + Piola/cofactor transport; this
is literally the Chapter 2.1 polynomial constructor with `(μ, ν) = (1, 0)` [verified].

**Denominator.** 18 terms, irreducible over Q [verified], an eigenfunction of the weighted field.

**Numerators [verified].** `N0 = Y0 X1 X2^3 (X0^2 − 3Y0^2)/24`, `N3 = −X0 X1 X2^3 (3X0^2 − Y0^2)/24`,
`N2 = A X2/6`, `N5 = A Y2/6`, `N1 = N4 = 0`. `div N = A`.

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* `W = (1/6)(2R ∂_R − S ∂_S + X2 ∂_{X2} + Y2 ∂_{Y2})` (weights `R:1/3, S:−1/6, X2,Y2:1/6, X1,Y1:0`)
  satisfies `W(C) = 0` and `W(D) = D` [verified in the original coordinates]. The crossed terms are
  admissible precisely because each has weight 0 (in `C`) or 1 (in `D`): e.g. `S·X1^3X2` has weight
  `−1/6 + 1/6 = 0`, `R·X2^4` has `1/3 + 2/3 = 1`.
* Density `b = X1 X2^3/8` has `W(b) = b/2`; `div W = 1/2` in `(R,S,X1,X2,Y1,Y2)`, so `div(bW) = b`;
  `(bW)(F) = b·D`. Piola transport through `(X0,Y0) ↦ (R,S)` (Jacobian `8X0Y0`, adjugate `[[2Y0,2Y0],[−2X0,2X0]]`)
  gives `N = adj(dΦ)·(bW)`, `A = 8X0Y0·b = X0 X1 X2^3 Y0`, `N(F) = N(D) = A·D`, `div N = A`.
* In the §2.1 notation: `D₀ = W(G) = D`, `W(D₀) = D₀` so `μ + ν = 1, μν = 0`, and the remainder
  `μνAF` vanishes — strictness is structural, not accidental.
* The "root-cube" form `αR^3 + κRS^2` is the alternative to Sylvester's `αR^3 + βS^3`: here the adapted
  coordinates are rational (a rational root factor `u − w`), so no descent is needed.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2722 B; `Witness.lean` 25770 B / 394 lines; coprimality via `z = X1`
(`F ∋ X1^6`), `fFiber = z^6 + 2z^4 + (202/103)z^3 + (511/103)z^2 + 3066/103`, `dFiber = 13`.

**Related.** `family-sylvester-norm` (same block idea, Hessian split instead of root), `family-shear`
(same constructor), `cofactor-mixed` (quadratic–cubic map). Open instances: none on this variety.
`four-plus-two-residual` is the natural place to try weighted derivations with crossed terms, but its core
`H(X0,X1,Y0,Y1)` has monomials (`X0^5X1, X0^3X1Y0Y1, X0X1^5, X0X1Y0^3Y1, Y0^5Y1, Y0Y1^5`) that are not
weight-homogeneous for any diagonal weighting I tried by inspection — no transfer is claimed.

## 5. family-shear  ("Logarithmic shear · chain")

**(F, q, A).** `F = X0^6 + 2X0^4Y0^2 + 3X0^2Y0^4 + X0 X2^5 + X1^6 + Y0^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6 = X0·T + D`,
`T = X0^5 + 2X0^3Y0^2 + 3X0Y0^4 + X2^5`, `D = X1^6 + Y0^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6` [verified]; `q = 1`,
`A = −10 X1 X2^4 Y1`.

**Symmetry [verified].** Sign group order 32: `X1, Y0, Y1, Y2` individually, `(X0,X2)` jointly.
Permutations: `S_3` on `{X1, Y1, Y2}`. `D` (a Fermat quartet) is invariant.

**Method.** Constructive, strict, `d = 6`, `Q = 0`. Chapter 2.1 cofactor transport through a
**triangular weighted polynomial map**, with `(μ, ν) = (1, 0)` [verified].

**Denominator.** `D = X1^6 + Y0^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6`, 4 terms, irreducible over Q (Fermat), independent of
`X0, X2`.

**Numerators [verified equal to `adj(dΦ)·P(Φ)`].** `N_i = −(5/3) X1 X2^4 Y1 · Z_i` for `i ∈ {0,1,3,4,5}`
and `N2 = X1 Y1 (6X0^5 + 12X0^3Y0^2 + 18X0Y0^4 + X2^5)/3`. All six nonzero. `div N = A`.

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* `Φ = (X0, T, X1^2, Y0, Y1, Y2)` with weights `(1,5,2,1,1,1)`, `Σ m_i = 11`; in target coordinates
  `G = x·t + s^3 + y0^6 + y1^6 + y2^6`, a product of degrees one and five plus a complementary weighted sextic.
* Weight-zero derivation `w = (x/6)∂_x − (t/6)∂_t + (s/3)∂_s + (1/6)(y0∂_{y0} + y1∂_{y1} + y2∂_{y2})`:
  the product `xt` is invariant (opposite weights), `w(G) = s^3 + Σ y^6 = D₀`, `w(D₀) = D₀` (`μ = 1, ν = 0`);
  `div w = 5/6` and density `b = Y1` (weight `12 − 11 = 1`) has `w(b) = b/6`, so `div(bw) = b`.
* `Δ = det dΦ = −10 X1 X2^4` [verified]; `A = b·Δ = −10 X1 X2^4 Y1` — the target is *defined* by the
  Jacobian density of the map, which is why the odd coefficient `−10` is in the instance. `N = adj(dΦ)·(bw)(Φ)`
  reproduces the record exactly; `N(F) = N(D) = A·D`, `div N = A`.
* The point of the example is that `T` depends on `X0, Y0, X2`, so `T` is independent of `X0` only in the
  adapted coordinates; the triangular Jacobian handles that dependence exactly, with no six-variable solve.
* Name clash: the book's "shear construction" (`TransitiveCubic.Shear`, by `fqlx`) is a different device
  (determinant-one coordinate shear for transitive paired-sign cubics). This instance's "shear" is the
  opposite-weight scaling of the product `X0·T`.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2656 B; `Witness.lean` 19688 B / 345 lines (smallest); coprimality via `z = X0`
(`F ∋ X0^6`), `fFiber = z^6 + 2z^4 + 3z^2 + z + 4`, `dFiber = 4`.

**Related.** `cofactor-mixed` (quadratic–cubic nonlinear map; the `why.md` names it as the comparison),
`family-root-cube` (same constructor). Open instances: none on this variety and none of the sixteen open
`F`'s has a degree-one-times-degree-five product structure.

## 6. family-sylvester-norm  ("Sylvester · rational norm")

**(F, q, A).** `F = p(u,v) + H(X1,Y1) + K(X2,Y2)` with `p = u^3 + 2u^2v + 3uv^2 + 5v^3`
(`(u,v) = (X0^2, Y0^2)`, `disc p = −367`), `H = 2X1^6 + 3X1^4Y1^2 + 5X1^2Y1^4 + 7Y1^6`,
`K = 11X2^6 + 13X2^4Y2^2 + 17X2^2Y2^4 + 19Y2^6` (12 terms); `q = 1`, `A = X0 X1^2 X2^2 Y0`.

**Symmetry [verified].** Coordinatewise even (order 64); no coordinate permutations (all three blocks
have different coefficients, by design).

**Method.** Constructive, strict, **`d = 12`** (the only non-sextic denominator in the group), `Q = 0`.
Sylvester diagonalization of the binary cubic over the Hessian field `Q(√1101)`, weighted partial Euler
field upstairs, then **trace of the fields (not of the numerators)**; the rational denominator is a norm.

**Denominator [verified].** Hessian covariant `H_p = (3ac−b^2)u^2 + (9ad−bc)uv + (3bd−c^2)v^2 =
5u^2 + 39uv + 21v^2`, `Δ_H = disc H_p = 1101 = −3·disc p`. Over `Q(√1101)`:
`H_p = 5 R S`, `R = u + ((39 − √1101)/10) v`, `S = u + ((39 + √1101)/10) v`,
`p = α R^3 + β S^3`, `α = 1/2 + 97√1101/6606`, `β = 1/2 − 97√1101/6606`, and `αβ R^3 S^3 = H_p^3/(9·1101)`.
Then
`D = H^2 + p H + H_p^3/(9 Δ_H) = (H + αR^3)(H + βS^3) = Norm_{Q(√1101)/Q}(H + αR^3)`,
30 terms, irreducible over Q, coefficient denominators `9909 = 9·1101`, `1101`, `367`.

**Numerators [record, verified].** degree 13; `N2 = N5 = 0` (`X2, Y2` never move); `N0, N1, N3, N4`
nonzero; `div N = A·(p + 4H)/2` (≠ `A`, as expected for an average of two eigenfields).

**Mechanism (5–10 lines).**
* Upstairs, group `αR^3` with `H` and `βS^3` with `K`: `D₊ = H + αR^3`, and the partial weighted Euler
  field `E = (1/6)(2R ∂_R + X1 ∂_{X1} + Y1 ∂_{Y1})` has `E(D₊) = D₊`, `E(βS^3 + K) = 0`, `div E = 2/3`;
  the density `b ∝ X1^2 X2^2` has `E(b) = b/3`, so `div(bE) = b`. Piola transport through `(X0,Y0) ↦ (R,S)`
  (Jacobian `∝ X0Y0`) realizes `A = X0Y0 · X1^2 X2^2`. This is again the §2.1 constructor with `μ = 1,
  ν = 0`, but over the quadratic field [mechanism per record; the Chapter 2.1 bookkeeping is mine].
* The conjugate field (swap `R ↔ S`, `α ↔ β`, `H ↔ K` roles) serves the same rational `A`; averaging the
  two **fields** keeps both strict equations, and the common denominator is `D₊ D₋ = D`. Tracing numerators
  without their denominators would not work — the record is explicit about this.
* So the price of rationality is the degree doubling 6 → 12; the record does not claim minimality.

**Lean.** `Solution.lean` 2832 B; `Witness.lean` 38168 B / 405 lines (largest: 30-term `D`, degree-13 `N`);
`maxHeartbeats` still 2000000 (sufficient); coprimality via `z = X2` (`F ∋ 11X2^6`, lc 11),
`fFiber = 11z^6 + 13z^4 + 17z^2 + 47`, `dFiber = 4991309/9909`.

**Related.** `family-root-cube` (root adaptation vs Hessian adaptation of the same kind of block),
`one-reflection` (`fqlx`; the book's §2.5 "binary-cubic Hessian construction" averages a field with its
`√δ`-conjugate "including its denominator", degree 30 halves, product 60 — the same descent principle at a
much larger scale). Open instances: none on this variety.

## 7. Cross-cutting observations, evidence, conjectures, failed attempts

**Proven / verified facts.**
1. All six certificates are strict and were re-verified exactly (sympy): `E1 = E2 = 0`, degrees, `D ≠ 0`,
   `gcd_Q(F, D) = 1`, parities. (`/tmp/hodge/scratch/verify6.py`.)
2. Three of the six (`root-cube`, `shear`, `passive-contact`) satisfy `div N = A`, i.e. the field annihilates
   `F − D`; `root-cube` and `shear` are exact instances of the §2.1 polynomial constructor with `(μ,ν)=(1,0)`;
   `sylvester-norm` is the same constructor over `Q(√1101)` followed by a norm. `block-cube` and `hesse-web`
   are not single-constructor fields (`div N = (1681/1701)A` and `X0Y0 a^2` respectively).
3. The coprimality proofs are uniform and elementary (F monic in a variable absent from D); no resultant or
   `AlgebraicSpecialization` machinery is used.
4. `passive-contact` is the only one of the six with a denominator reducible over Q, and its pole along the
   rational quadric factor is genuine.
5. Smoothness and legality over ℂ (Singular 4.x in `ddg-cas:latest`, Gröbner bases over Q, `dp` order):
   `dim std(jacob F) = 0` in the affine cone for **all six** `F` (so each `V(F) ⊂ P^5` is a smooth sextic),
   and `dim std(F, D) = 4` for all six pairs (`V(F,D)` has codimension 2 in A^6, i.e. no common factor,
   over Q and hence over ℂ since the Gröbner dimension is field-extension invariant). This independently
   confirms the Lean `denominator_legal` theorems. (`/tmp/hodge/scratch/smooth6.sing`; the run took about
   12 minutes, dominated by the 26-term root-cube Jacobian ideal.)

**Computational evidence / numerology.**

* hesse-web: tangency parameters `C^3 + 2C^2 − 4 = 0`, flex tangent `C = 2/3`, tangency points `(1:1:−2/C)`.
