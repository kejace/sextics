## Method (what is new in wave 2)
* Exact E1 families per G_aff character sector: (J:A)_d[chi] = {D in S_d[chi] : A D in J_{d+6}} computed over Q
  (python-flint fmpz nullspace), split into the Jacobian part J_d[chi] and the non-Jacobian extras.
* Koszul-form variable projection: for D(a) = sum a_k D_k with E1-lifts N_k, every E1 solution is
  N = sum a_k N_k + K, K = sum_{r<s} Q_rs (F_s e_r - F_r e_s); the certificate reduces to B_D(N) = F Q.
  f(a) = min over (Q_rs, Q) of ||B_{D(a)}(sum a_k N_k + K) - F Q||^2, minimized on the sphere (L-BFGS, exact
  Golub-Pereyra gradient), Q-block eliminated by projection; spurious directions (D(a) = 0, D(a) in F*S_{d-6})
  projected out.  Sector restriction of all unknowns is lossless for eigen-denominators and makes every
  sector system small (hundreds to a few thousand rows).  Mixed-character / mixed-parity modes are also supported.
* Any minimizer with relative residual < 1e-10 is rationally reconstructed in monomial coordinates with
  increasing denominators and re-solved EXACTLY (engine fixed_d / symmetry solvers + core.verify_witness +
  coprimality check).  Only an exact re-solve counts.
