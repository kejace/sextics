# Part 5 — The elliptic-family (E5) route in W-language, what a Lean proof would need, and the precise failure points

## 5.1 Where V_1 lives geometrically
W is swept by a 3-dimensional family 𝓕 of weighted lines L (rbajaj5's free line, companion solve).  For L ⊄ B,
E_L = π^{-1}(L) = {X2^3 = c_L(s,t)} is a j = 0 plane cubic with μ3 acting as CM.  The universal curve 𝓔 → 𝓕 is an
isotrivial elliptic fibration dominating X, so H^4(X) ↪ H^4(𝓔) and (modulo N^1)
   V_1 ⊕ V_2 ↪ H^3(𝓕, L_χ ⊕ L_χ̄),   L_χ = (R^1)_χ a rank-one local system of finite monodromy.
Base change to 𝓕' trivialising L_χ: V_1 ⊕ V_2 ⊂ H^3(𝓕')_{χ'} ⊗ H^1(E_0).  The (4,0)-class Ω/F sits in
H^{3,0}(𝓕')_{χ'} ⊗ H^{1,0}(E_0) (so h^{3,0}(𝓕')_{χ'} ≥ 1 and 𝓕' is not uniruled), the τ-invariant (3,1)-part
(107 classes) in H^{2,1}(𝓕')_{χ'} ⊗ H^{1,0}(E_0).

## 5.2 Supports predicted by this picture (refining dossier E5)
A class ω ⊗ η with ω ∈ N^1 H^3(𝓕')_{χ'} (i.e. ω supported on a surface S ⊂ 𝓕') is divisor-supported on 𝓕' × E_0 by
S × E_0, hence on X by the divisor T_S = ∪_{L ∈ S} E_L — the 3-fold swept by the elliptic curves over a surface of lines.
Its resolution is an isotrivial elliptic surface-fibration over S' (a cover of S) and h^{2,0}(T̃_S)_ζ ≈ q(S')_χ: the
twisted irregularity of the surface of lines.  So: the supports of V_1 are exactly the "π^{-1}(scroll of lines over an
irregular surface of 𝓕)" divisors, and Part 4's triple-point curves are their degenerate shadow (a line tangent to B to
order 3 at a point P ∈ Γ gives the Ẽ6 fibre).  The GHC for the threefold 𝓕' (coniveau of the τ-invariant χ'-part of
H^3, which has no (3,0)-part) is exactly the statement that enough such surfaces exist — open in general; it is known
for uniruled/rationally connected threefolds (Bloch–Srinivas) which 𝓕' is not.
Natural candidate surfaces S ⊂ 𝓕: lines meeting a fixed surface Σ ⊂ W (a 2-dim family; Σ = W ∩ {ℓ = ℓ' = 0}), lines
tangent to B (the discriminant surface of the elliptic family), lines meeting Γ_X-type curves.  Their twisted irregularity
is computable only after 𝓕 itself is computed (incidence of 5 linear forms + 1 binary cubic modulo GL2: a Gröbner
computation in ~17 unknowns over F_p, hours on the remote) — not done here.

## 5.3 Hodge-theoretic lower bound (what would make Part 4 at low degree hopeless)
If the Q-Hodge structure on S_A ⊕ S̄_A is simple, every support needs Σ h^{2,0}(E_i) ≥ 67 (dossier E4) and the
degree-9 families of Part 4 (≤ 20 twisted forms) cannot work regardless of E2.  Algebraically computable evidence: the
sub-VHS generated by ξ over the G-symmetric family has (2,2)-part ⊇ Def_G·ξ and (1,3)-part ⊇ Def_G^2·ξ (multiplication in
the Jacobian ring by the 41 G-invariant deformation directions) — their dimensions are lower bounds for h^{1,1}(T̃) and
for the (1,3)-part of any support of ξ on a GENERIC member; they do not bound h^{2,0} without the (1,−1)-part of the
connection, which is transcendental.  Not computed in this session (would need J_12, J_18 normal forms per sector on the
remote).

## 5.4 What a Lean proof via the shared library would need (and why none of the four library routes fits)
(1) Explicit witness (leangen template): needs D of degree ≥ 9 (rigorous lower bound 7, probably 9 after relax8) with
    E2 consistency — none found; the candidate families of Part 4 are the right shape but their E2 systems are
    inconsistent at degree 8 (all plane families, sealed) and, so far, at degree 9 (Part 4.4).
(2) Reflection/quotient + polynomial-family integration (companion's route): needs a uniruled quotient whose averaging
    ideal contains ξ — excluded for every quotient of X, of the Galois closure Z̃ (128 subgroups) and of the P^2×P^2
    closure (Part 1), with margins deg K ≥ 0 (vs < 0 needed).
(3) §4.6 descent (holdout route): needs an exchange cover with Fano-fibred quotient — no §2.7 exchange exists for any
    linear form (Part 2, rigorous), and the natural exchange σ gives deg K = 1.
(4) Surface-fibration sector theorem (§4.2 route): fibre quotients have p_g ≥ 2 (Part 3).
A fifth route — "elliptic-companion" style transport along the isotrivial curves E_L — would integrate a twisted source
along genus-one fibres: the primitive is an elliptic integral, not rational; the library's polynomial-source theorem and
EllipticCompanions (which handle u^3 = p splittings, absent here) do not cover it.  This is the precise sense in which
the lane "isolates target transport from the machinery for rational-curve families" (why.md).

## 5.5 Bottom line for the construct lens
Every published mechanism fails by representation theory (Parts 1–3; computations exact).  The only mechanism that
reaches the ζ3-character on a divisor is the isotrivial j = 0 elliptic surface mechanism (Part 4 / 5.2), which is
genuinely about X (not W).  Its certificate-level realisation requires either (a) the Fano variety of lines 𝓕 and an
irregular surface of lines, or (b) high-degree denominators with several triple-point curves inside B; both are beyond
the degree range (≤ 10) where sector solvers are routine.  Recommended next computation for the lane: compute 𝓕
(remote, Gröbner over F_p) and the twisted irregularity of the surface of lines tangent to B; if positive, derive the
sweep divisor's equation and test it with the sparse sector solver.
