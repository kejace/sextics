**Summary of the failure of each book mechanism (why.md, verified against the chapters):**
1. *Reflection theorem (§3.3)*: F even in X2 and Y2; A even in both ⇒ residue anti-invariant ⇒ not served. Pulling back to the reflection quotient by Y2 kills exactly the σ-anti-invariant part of the binary factor that the target needs (benediamond's character computation, §3).
2. *Split theorem (3A.1–3A.3)*: needs 3+3 (ternary, ternary). The 4+2 triple cover T_H^3 = H ⊂ P(1,1,1,1,2) has K = O(0) by weighted adjunction (degree 6 = 1+1+1+1+2), so it is a Calabi–Yau threefold, not Fano; the binary companion W_B is K3. "Merely carrying over the rational-curve argument therefore does not establish support."
3. *Binary-block theorem (§2a)*: needs three decoupled binary blocks.
4. *Subset-Euler (§2.2)*: degree mismatch (6 vs 2, 0 vs 4).
5. *Paired-sign / surface-quotient theorem (§4.2)*: F is not in the 26-coefficient Γ-invariant space (coupling X0^3X1Y0Y1 is not paired-sign invariant under s0: (X0,Y0)→(−X0,−Y0)? it is: degree in (X0,Y0) is 4 — even; under s1: degree in (X1,Y1) is 2 — even. Actually both coupling monomials ARE paired-sign invariant; but the Y-block g(Y0,Y1)=Y0^6+(5/7)Y0^5Y1+(15/7)Y0Y1^5+Y1^6 has monomials of (X0,Y0)-degree 5 — odd — so s0 is broken; see §1.3.) 
6. *Hessian symmetrizer*: dimension 2, so no hidden linear 3+3 or 2+2+2 split.
