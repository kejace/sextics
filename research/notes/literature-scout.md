## Findings (filled in as found)

### A. The generalized Hodge conjecture for sextic fourfolds with involution (Voisin's question; Diamond)

**A1. B. E. Diamond, "Hodge Structures in Sextic Fourfolds Equipped with an Involution", arXiv:2603.29157, v1 2026-03-31, v2 2026-08-10 ("various typo-fixes and minor improvements").** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29157 , HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29157v2
- Statement: for F = f(U0,U1,U2) + f(V0,V1,V2) (Shioda construction attached to a smooth plane sextic curve f) with involution iota, the iota-invariant part H of H^4(X,Q) has Hodge coniveau 1; the paper proves the GHC prediction (a divisor Y with H in ker(H^4(X)->H^4(X-Y))) when the Waring rank of f is the minimum 3, i.e. f = L1^6+L2^6+L3^6 (an 8-dimensional projective family of plane sextics inside the 27-dimensional set of smooth ones). The author states the techniques reach at least 17 projective dimensions of invariant sextics out of 235 (8 of them Shioda-Katsura type); "pushing further seems plausible".
- The algebraic reformulation (the hodge.computer contract): for every q in {0..4} and every anti-invariant A in R^{6q}, a rational vector field v with homogeneous components of degree 6q-5, denominators not in (F), such that F^{q+1} | A - q dF(v) + F div(v). Theorem 2.7 (vanishing criterion), Theorem 2.13 (algebraic primitive via Grothendieck comparison, needs a finite pole-order hypothesis along X). Remark 2.6: only a weak form of Deligne's degeneration is implied. Remark 3.7: the combinatorial fact behind the Fermat-specific Construction 3.9 fails as soon as q > 2.
- Explicitly open in the paper: (i) Voisin's question on a nonempty Zariski-open set of invariant sextics ("doing as much for general F seems difficult"); (ii) "parameterized by 1-cycles" equivalent to geometric coniveau 1 for ALL smooth sextics (would follow from the Lefschetz standard conjecture; Voisin has it for general sextics); (iii) algebraic primitive without finite pole order (Section 2.3, "proves apparently essential"); (iv) exact maximum Waring rank of ternary sextics. Collected by Emergent Mind: https://www.emergentmind.com/open-problems/algebraic-primitive-without-finite-pole-order-hypothesis and https://www.emergentmind.com/open-problems/equivalence-parameterized-by-1-cycles-vs-geometric-coniveau-for-all-smooth-sextics
- Lanes: all 16 (it is the source of the contract). Directly useful: the 17-dimensional reach is the baseline the book already exceeds (Ch. 4 records 107-dim all-target families), so no new mechanism here beyond the book; but the remark that the Fermat construction's combinatorics fail for q>2 is consistent with cycle-q2 (q=2) being the hardest open lane. No citing papers found yet (Semantic Scholar rate-limited; re-check below).

### B. Kuga-Satake algebraicity and Weil-type fourfolds (lane polarized-k3-remainder)

**B1. E. Markman, "Secant sheaves and Weil classes on abelian varieties", arXiv:2509.23403 (Sept 2025).** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23403 - Weil classes are algebraic on ALL abelian fourfolds of Weil type (every imaginary quadratic field, every discriminant; the discriminant-1 case is arXiv:2502.03415 "Cycles on abelian 2n-folds of Weil type from secant sheaves on abelian n-folds", with the general case via a Schoen degeneration). Consequence (stated in the abstract): the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties of dimension <= 5.
**B2. S. Floccari, "K3 surfaces associated to varieties of generalized Kummer type", arXiv:2501.02315 (Jan 2025)** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02315 - Kuga-Satake algebraic and Hodge conjecture for powers for the K3 surfaces attached to Kum_n-type varieties (Picard rank >= 16 families). **B3. Floccari-Fu, "The Hodge conjecture for Weil fourfolds with discriminant 1 via singular OG6-varieties", arXiv:2504.13607** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13607 - reduces Weil fourfolds to Kuga-Satake for the K3 surfaces S_K. **B4. Floccari, Compositio 160 (2024), arXiv:2210.02948**: infinitely many families of K3 surfaces of general Picard rank 16 satisfying the Kuga-Satake Hodge conjecture.
- Lane relevance: polarized-k3-remainder needs Lambda^2 of the rank-21 transcendental lattice of a double plane with rho <= 4 (probe: NS disc -211, Aut(C_f)=1). None of B1-B4 reaches Picard rank < 16 K3 surfaces; the Kuga-Satake Hodge conjecture for a general degree-2 K3 remains open as of this scan. Bolognesi-Laterveer (arXiv:2310.11981, order-3 non-symplectic automorphism with a fixed curve) remains the only applicable mechanism and the book already uses it (Ch. 3a, 3A.6). Conclusion for the lane: no literature shortcut; the instance needs its own geometry.

### C. Pryms and cyclic covers (lanes four-plus-two-residual, cyclic-triple-residual, symmetric-sextic-residual)

**C1. E. M. Rains, "The monodromy of cyclic Pryms", arXiv:2512.19597 (Dec 2025, 49 pp.)** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19597 - geometric monodromy of Pryms of cyclic covers in natural one-parameter families is sandwiched between a unitary group and its derived subgroup; applied to elliptic surfaces with j=0 (degree-6 cyclic covers, Z[zeta3]) and j=1728. Relevance: four-plus-two-residual's binary block u^3 = B(X2,Y2) gives a genus-4 cyclic triple cover whose Prym was found absolutely simple with End^0 = Q(zeta3) (probe 2026-11-01); Rains's unitary-monodromy statement is the generic-case explanation (a general member of the family has no extra endomorphisms), so no decomposition can be expected without choosing a special B. Confirms the probe; does not open a route.
**C2. A. Shatsila, "Theta-duality and Prym-Torelli for cyclic covers of hyperelliptic curves", arXiv:2609.14135 (Sept 2026).** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14135 - Prym-Torelli for etale cyclic covers of odd prime degree of hyperelliptic curves; d=3 via trigonal pencils. Not directly usable.
**C3. T. Alexandrou, arXiv:2110.02093 (2021)** - generic isogeny decomposition of the Prym of a cyclic branched cover (the Z[zeta_d]-eigen-decomposition); background for the (2,1)-signature count used in the four-plus-two probe.

### D. Computational Hodge-conjecture certificates (context for hodge.computer)

**D1. R. Jumagulov, "The Hodge conjecture for Fermat fourfolds of odd degree at most 199", arXiv:2608.18134 (July 2026, 19 pp.)** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18134 - computer-assisted proof via three closure criteria (*-split closure: characters splitting into two zero-sum triples are images of divisor classes on X^1_m x X^1_m under a joining-line correspondence; two-pair standard closure via Aoki standard sextuples; level-lifted closure, e.g. m=33 lifted to m=66 and descended along X^4_66 -> X^4_33) plus a census of 78,299 Galois-orbit representatives; ancillary code and "machine-checked certificates" (the comments field mentions a Lean formalisation; the body text does not describe how). Answers da Silva's Question 1 negatively at m=33. Relevance: degree 6 is even and not treated; the (2,2) Hodge-class problem on Fermat is different from the coniveau-1 (3,1)-support problem here. Technique transfer: the "level-lifting then descend along a finite cover" pattern is exactly the Ch. 4 q-raising/deck-descent idea and could be relevant to lanes whose F is a pullback (quadratic-cubic-pullback, cycle-q2). Low priority.
