## 1. The obstruction and the geometric mechanism a certificate needs

### 1.1 Data
`F = sum_{i=0}^5 Q_i^3` with the six globally Y-even quadrics (verified exactly: F - sum Q_i^3 = 0)

```
Q0 = X0^2 + 5/7 (X1X2 + Y0Y1)     Q3 = Y0^2 + 5/17 (X0X1 + Y1Y2)
Q1 = X1^2 + 5/11(X0X2 + Y1Y2)     Q4 = Y1^2 + 5/19 (X1X2 + Y0Y2)
Q2 = X2^2 + 5/13(X0X1 + Y0Y2)     Q5 = Y2^2 + 5/23 (X0X2 + Y0Y1)
```
phi = (Q_0..Q_5): P^5_Z -> P^5_U is finite flat of degree 32 (base-point free since mod 5 it is the
squaring map), X = phi^{-1}(C), C = Fermat cubic fourfold.  phi factors through X/tau -> C (degree 16).
delta = det(dQ_i/dZ_j) is Y-odd of degree 6 (64 terms); R = V(delta) cap X is the ramification divisor
(class 6H on X), its image B = phi(R) is the branch divisor (degree 48 in U-space, since R -> B is 2:1
through tau).  Everything is a 5-adically small deformation of the Fermat sextic F_0 = sum Z_i^6
(Q_i = Z_i^2 + 5 * (cross terms)).

### 1.2 Why the known machinery stops
* Piola/weighted-Fano (book 3.4): serves only the determinant density delta (b constant at q=1).
  [A] is independent of [delta] in the Jacobian ring (mod 5 these are distinct Fermat monomials).
* f_* C_X = C + L with L of rank 31; the residual invariant (3,1) space (dim 207, benediamond's
  count 226 - 18 - 1) lives in H^4(C, L).  For the deformed map the monodromy is generated by the
  transpositions around the (presumably irreducible) branch divisor, so L is the standard
  representation of S_32.  The intransitive quotients of the Galois closure (X itself, the symmetric
  fibre square Y' = closure of X x_C X minus the diagonal, ...) are of general type:
  K_{Y'} = pr_1^* K_X + (ramification of pr_1) = pr_2^*(R) = pr_2^* O(6), big.  So the averaging-ideal
  / uniruled-quotient route of book 3.1-3.3 is closed exactly as in book 4.7 (V_std obstruction).
  Only a direct divisor support with non-rational singularities (book 3.1) can work.
* Pulled-back divisors f^{-1}(Z), Z = C cap V(G): at a point where f is etale, singularities of Z
  pull back unchanged.  Hyperplane sections of C through an Eckardt point (e.g. U_0 + U_3 = 0) are
  cones over a cubic surface: rational.  A quasi-homogeneous singularity is rational iff the weight
  sum exceeds 1; pulling a cubic cone back through k independent local square roots gives weights
  k*(1/6) + (4-k)/3, which is <= 1 only for k >= 2, i.e. only at corank >= 2 ramification points,
  a finite set on X.  So isolated-point mechanisms are generically useless here.
* Cones (D in few variables) with vertex a plane over a cubic curve: the transversal singularity is
  simple elliptic along the plane sextic curve Pi cap X, so h^{2,0} of the resolved divisor is >= 9,
  but the computed served spaces are trivial for every such D (section 2): the Gysin images land in
  the tau-anti-invariant sector (tau acts by -1 on H^{1,0} of the tangent-cone cubic for vertex planes
  {X=0}, {X0=X1=Y0=0}, ...) or vanish.  Cones over surfaces with vertex a point (Fermat: X0^2+Y0^2)
  serve products m_2(other 4 variables) * (quartic in the two vertex variables), never X0^3 Y0^3.

### 1.3 The Fermat limit and the join mechanism (the one that works at F_0)
On F_0 the target X0^3 Y0^3 has the strict subset-Euler certificate (book 2.2)

    v = X0^3 Y0^3 * E_X / (6 (X0^6 + X1^6 + X2^6)),   E_X = X0 d_X0 + X1 d_X1 + X2 d_X2,

(verified exactly with core.verify_witness, see log).  Its support is V(X0^6+X1^6+X2^6) cap X_0 =
the JOIN of the two plane sextic curves Gamma_X = {X-block = 0, Y = 0}, Gamma_Y; it is singular along
both curves with transversal type "cone over a plane sextic" (local p_g = 20), and the served
classes are the products m_1(X) m_2(Y), deg m_1 = deg m_2 = 3, which contain X0^3 Y0^3.
In the cover picture this is the pull-back of the join of two plane Fermat cubics
{U_0^3+U_1^3+U_2^3 = 0 = U_3^3+U_4^3+U_5^3} subset C.

For the deformed F the same support is D_S = sum_{i in S} Q_i^3, |S| = 3 (ten supports, each shared
by S and its complement).  f^{-1}(join) is singular along C_1 = f^{-1}(Gamma_1) (a curve of degree 48,
genus ~145: a 32-sheeted cover of the plane cubic with ~288 simple branch points over B cap Gamma_1;
tau-quotient genus ~73) with transversal type cone-over-cubic = simple elliptic (non-rational).  The
exceptional elliptic surface is C_1 x Gamma_2 (trivial fibration, Hodge bundle trivial), so the
resolved divisor has h^{2,0} = g(C_1) + g(C_2) ~ 290, tau-invariant part ~146.  These forms are NOT
pulled back from the resolution of the join downstairs (which has h^{2,0} = 2); their Gysin images have
zero trace to C, i.e. they land in the residual 207-dimensional sector.  This is precisely the
"resolution of non-rational singularities of a pull-back contributes extra H^2(O)" loophole left open
in benediamond's conditional obstruction.  Whether [A] lies in the span (of one split, or of the sum
over the ten splits, which would give a bundle certificate via HasCertificate.add with ten degree-6
pieces) is the main computation of this attack (section 2.4).

### 1.4 Rigid facts from the E1 kernel sweep
For d <= 7 the kernel of D -> [A D] on S_d^even -> R_{d+6}^odd equals J(F)_d^even exactly (mod p
computation; the rank of the J-block equals its Hilbert-function value, so the mod-p bound is an exact
upper bound over Q).  Consequences (rigorous): no certificate with deg D <= 4 exists for this target;
any denominator of degree 5, 6 or 7 lies in the Jacobian ideal J(F).
