## 2.2 The averaging-ideal test (book §3.1) in this language

H^4(Z̃) ⊇ Ind_N^{B3}(H^4(X)) is not what we need; rather H^4(X) = H^4(Z̃)^N and the B3-module generated by the pull-back of
H^4(X) is a quotient of Q[B3/N] ⊗ H^4(X).  Q[B3/N] = (3,∅) ⊕ (21,∅) ⊕ (2,1) = 1 ⊕ (2-dim S3-standard) ⊕ V, V the
3-dimensional reflection representation; the N-fixed vectors in the three summands have s1-sign +, +, − respectively.
Since s1 ∈ M acts on H^4(X) = H^4(Z̃)^N as the deck involution s of X → W, the s-odd part of H^4(X) (the residual sector of
rcr and qcr, which contains ξ) is the multiplicity space of V:  V ⊗ H^4(X)^{s=−} ⊂ H^4(Z̃).  The extra symmetries
commuting with the tower (ρ̃ for rcr; r1, G_diag for qcr) act on the multiplicity space; ξ is an eigenvector with the
signs given by the dossiers (rcr: ρ = −1; qcr: r1 = −1, G_diag = +1).

Reaching criterion.  A subgroup K ⊂ B3 × (extra) reaches ξ iff (V ⊗ line(ξ))^K ≠ 0 iff there is v ∈ V∖0 with
b·v = λ(t)^{−1} v for all (b, t) ∈ K, λ(t) the sign of t on ξ.  Maximal reaching subgroups, one per B3-orbit type of v:
| v | Stab_{B3}(v) | #reflections | deg K_{Z̃/K} by (★) | Z̃/K | twisted versions (b, t) with bv = λ(t)^{-1}v |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| e1 | N ≅ D4 | 4 | 0 | X | ⟨N, (s1, t)⟩ with λ(t) = −1: X/(s·t) — rcr: X/τ (K ≡ 0); qcr: X/(s0 r1), X/(s0 σ0 r1 …) — all residue-preserving ⇒ κ = 0 (Reid–Tai, fqlx/dossier §3.3) |
| e1 + e2 | ⟨s3, (12)⟩ | 2 | 2 | (4,6)-CI in P(1^6, 2) | adding (−1, t) or (s1s2, t) with λ(t) = −1: quotient of the CI by an involution without fixed divisor on Z̃ ⇒ K still 2H |
| e1 + e2 + e3 | S3 | 3 | 1 | Z̃/S3 (birational to a degree-8 hypersurface in P^5) | adding (−1, t): K still H |
| e1 + 2e2 (plane, non-diagonal) | ⟨s3⟩ | 1 | 3 | double cover type | — |
| generic | 1 | 0 | 4 | Z̃ | — |
Elements (b, t) with t a reflection of X commuting with the tower (qcr: t = r1) never enter a reaching K unless paired with
b acting as −1 on v, and such (b, r1) have fixed locus of codimension ≥ 2 on Z̃; so (★) is unchanged by the twists.

Conclusion (rcr: construct lens §2.2; qcr: NEW, same proof): every intermediate quotient of the B3-closure that sees the
residual class has deg K ≥ 0; the ones with K ≡ 0 are the quotients of X by residue-preserving subgroups (κ = 0), the
others have K ample.  Quotients of a klt variety are klt, and a klt variety with pseudo-effective K is not uniruled
(BDPP), so no uniruled (in particular no Fano) intermediate quotient reaches ξ, for any of the four qcr towers and for
the rcr tower.  The klt hypothesis needs Z̃ to have klt singularities: Z̃ is a (2,4,6) complete intersection; its
singular locus is being computed (remote job trio-qcr-ztsing; for rcr and ctr the analogous checks were queued by the
construct lenses).  The degree bookkeeping (★) does not depend on it.

Relation to Part 1.  Theorem 1 (rigidity) says that subgroups K ⊂ M = Gal(Z̃/W) can only reach ξ if K ⊂ N; the table
confirms it: the only M-subgroups in the table are N and its twists.  All genuinely new quotients (rows 2–5) come from
K ⊄ M, i.e. they use the S3-mixing of the three square roots — the "exchange" of the book's §4.6/§4.7 — and all of them
have K ≥ H.  This is the precise sense in which the B3-closure "almost" helps: it does produce varieties seeing the
residual class (unlike any abelian cover), but never uniruled ones.

Theorem 1 forces every reaching K ⊂ M to lie in N (the 38 include X, X/⟨s⟩, X/τ, …, all K ≡ 0), and the reaching K ⊄ M

intermediate quotient; the §4.6 descent criterion would need Z̃/⟨r,σ⟩ (deg K = 1) to be supported — no theorem applies.

## 2.4 Verdict of Part 2
* ctr, rcr: Galois-closure routes exhausted by the construct lenses (exact enumeration); Theorem 1 shows why only the
  σ-/S3-mixing quotients could ever appear and they have K ≥ H.
* qcr (new): the four real reflection towers all have the full B3 closure; the reaching quotients are X/H (κ = 0),
  the (4,6)-CI in P(1^6,2) (K = 2H) and Z̃/S3 (K = H); no uniruled quotient reaches ξ (modulo klt of Z̃, remote check).
* Residual possibility inside this circle of ideas: a reaching quotient with K = H or 2H whose H^4 is nevertheless
  divisor-supported for a reason other than uniruledness (e.g. a fibration in p_g = q = 0 surfaces).  For rcr the
  construct lens found p_g ≥ 9 canonical forms on both; for qcr the same count applies ((4,6)-CI: quadrics in
  P(1^6,2) odd/even bookkeeping gives ≥ 6 invariant forms; degree-8 model: 21 quadrics in P^5), so neither has
  p_g = 0 fibres over a surface in any obvious way.  Not pursued further.
