# wave2 construct — klein-coupled-character — part 1: framework (what a character-changing cover must look like)

Notation: (x,y,z,t,r,s) = (X0..Y2). F = K + M + G, K = x^5y + y^5z + z^5x, M = (5/7)(xy^3 tr + 2x^3z rs + 3yz^3 ts),
G = t^6+r^6+s^6. sigma = diag(zeta,zeta^2,zeta^4,1,1,1) (zeta^7=1), tau = (Y -> -Y). Target A = x^3 t^3: sigma-character 3,
tau-invariant residue. Sector of A: zeta^3-eigenspace of H^4_prim(X)^tau, Hodge (26,123,37).

## 1.1 Averaging-ideal test on an auxiliary cover (book 3.1 + 4.6, restated as a representation criterion)

Let p: Z -> X be proper dominant generically finite (Z smooth projective, everything resolved equivariantly),
Gamma a finite subgroup of Aut(Z), Delta = {d in Gamma : p d = p} (deck part), N = N_Gamma(Delta) (the elements
descending to automorphisms of X), K a subgroup of Gamma with H^4(Z/K) divisor-supported.
For xi in the zeta^3-sector put a = p^* xi in V = H^4(Z)/N^1. Then a is Delta-invariant and N acts on the
Q[N]-module generated by a through the inflated character chi~ (Galois orbit {3,5,6}; 6-dim over Q).
I = Q[Gamma] e_K Q[Gamma] kills V, so every simple constituent rho of M := Q[Gamma] a satisfies rho^K = 0.
CRITERION: if every simple constituent of Ind_N^Gamma(chi~) occurs in the permutation module Q[Gamma/K]
(Frobenius: rho^K != 0), then M = 0, i.e. p^* xi in N^1 H^4(Z) and (trace) xi in N^1 H^4(X).
Book 4.6 is the case Gamma = <d,e,sigma'> = (Z/n x Z/n) x| Z/2 (wreath), Delta = <d>, N = <d,e>, K = <sigma'> or
<r,sigma'>: Ind_N^Gamma(chi~) is the 2-dim rep spanned by a, sigma'a, which has the K-fixed vector a + sigma'a.
If K is contained in N the criterion reduces (Mackey) to quotients of X itself, which all kill character 3
(dossier 2.4); so THE EXCHANGE MUST NOT DESCEND TO X.

## 1.2 Fibre-product form of the 4.6 cover (what "deck + exchange" means geometrically)

With d, e = sigma' d sigma' commuting of order n, Z/<d> = X, Z/<e> = X' (isomorphic to X via sigma'),
Z/<d,e> = X/<g> =: W_g where g = image of e in Aut(X). sigma' normalizes <d,e>, hence induces an involution
g-bar of W_g, and Z is birational to the TWISTED fibre product X x_{W_g, g-bar} X (degree n^2 over W_g).
If g-bar lifted to an automorphism of X the fibre product would be a union of graphs (reducible, each
component birational to X) and the construction collapses (d = id). Hence:
  a 4.6-type cover for this lane = (g in <sigma,tau> with nontrivial sigma-part) + (a birational involution
  g-bar of X/<g> that does NOT lift to X) + (Z/<swap> or Z/<swap, r> divisor-supported).
Only two quotients exist: X/<sigma> (g = sigma^k) and W = X/<sigma,tau> (g = sigma^k tau, order 14; the
book's 4.3 weighted fourfold in P(2,2,2,1,1,1)).

## 1.3 Fibrewise (dihedral) form, as in holdout

holdout (book 2.7/4.6) is the special case: X -> P^1_{[Y0:Y2]} with CY-threefold fibres
{sum X^6 + y^6 + c T^6 + yT l(X) = 0}, c = alpha^6+beta^6; over the base cover C: alpha^6+beta^6 = 1 each fibre
acquires the swap iota: y <-> T; iota_{d b'} iota_{b'} = g = diag(zeta^-1, zeta, zeta^-1) on Y, iota g iota = g^-1
(dihedral D_6 on the geometric generic fibre, only Z/6 defined over k(P^1)); fibrewise quotient by <iota, r>
is the Q-Fano threefold {H = 0} in P(1,1,1,2,2) -> Z/<r,sigma'> uniruled over the curve C.
GENERAL PATTERN: a sigma-equivariant fibration X -> B with sigma trivial on B (B = P^2_Y, or P^1 of a Y-ratio),
a Galois cover B' -> B with group <d>, and a family of fibrewise involutions iota_{b'} with
iota_{d b'} iota_{b'} = g (a fixed element of <sigma,tau> with sigma-part != 1), so that the geometric generic
fibre has a dihedral group <g, iota> of which only <g> is defined over k(B); and the fibrewise quotient must give
a supported total space (Fano-fibration over a curve, or p_g = q = 0 surface fibres over a surface, book 3.2).

## 1.4 Consequence for the character: what g must be, and the two allowed shapes
g = sigma^k tau^j, k != 0. Av_g kills the zeta^3-sector, so the criterion needs nothing about X/<g>
for this sector (X/<g> supported would additionally give the trivial sector, which 4.3 already covers for g = sigma tau).
The exchange conjugates d (deck) to e (covering g); on fibres it conjugates g to g^-1; since
sigma^-1 = diag(zeta^6, zeta^5, zeta^3), an involution normalizing <sigma> with iota sigma iota = sigma^-1 must map the
eigenspaces (zeta, zeta^2, zeta^4) to (zeta^6, zeta^5, zeta^3), which are ABSENT among the fibre coordinates
(x,y,z) of every Y-ratio fibration. Hence no LINEAR fibrewise involution can realise the holdout pattern
(see part 2 for the full linear classification); any realisation must use a nonlinear (Cremona-type)
involution of the fibres or a cover not of base-change type.
