Hence `A ∂_w²F ∈ J_16` is necessary. The 21 classes `[A F_ij]` (i ≤ j) lie in 21 distinct characters of the order-72 diagonal group and each is nonzero in `R_16` (sector-wise ranks at two primes; `dim J_16 = 19398` as for a regular sequence). So `w_i w_j = 0` for all i, j, i.e. `w = 0`.
