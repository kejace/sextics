# Draft discussion comment for #61 (`cycle-q2`) — exclusion / rigor update (DRAFT, not posted)

**Scope.** Denominator-degree bounds and exact exclusions for `(F, q=2, A = X0²X1²X2³Y0²Y1²Y2)`; nothing here claims nonexistence of a certificate. All statements are about the cleared identity `A D² − 2D Σ N_i ∂_iF + F(D Σ ∂_iN_i − Σ N_i ∂_iD) = F³Q` (`deg N_i = deg D + 7`, `deg Q = 2 deg D − 6`), with `D` arbitrary (no parity or symmetry assumption unless stated).

**1. Proven: no certificate with `deg D ≤ 5`.** (Previously: `deg D ≤ 4` impossible, `deg D = 5` excluded only for `D = F_{X_i}`.)
* E1 (`A·D ∈ J(F)`) and `Ann_d([A]) = 0` for `d ≤ 5` give `deg D ≥ 5`, and at `d = 5` force `D = ∂_wF = Σ w_i F_i`, `w ∈ ℂ⁶` (Singular, `vdim J = 15625`, `Ann_d` recomputed at p = 2^31−1 and 10^9+7; zero kernels transfer to ℚ because the Jacobian ranks equal their regular-sequence values).
* "Hessian relaxation": with `N⁰ = (A/2)w` the E1 equation holds exactly, every other solution differs by a Koszul field `K = α·∇F` (regular sequence), the Euler-gauge term cancels identically, and E2 becomes `H(w) + (D div K − K·∇D) = 2F²Q` with `H(w) = (∂_wF)(∂_wA) − A ∂_w²F ≡ −A ∂_w²F (mod J)`. Hence `A ∂_w²F ∈ J_16` is necessary. The 21 classes `[A F_ij]` (i ≤ j) lie in 21 distinct characters of the order-72 diagonal group and each is nonzero in `R_16` (sector-wise ranks at two primes; `dim J_16 = 19398` as for a regular sequence). So `w_i w_j = 0` for all i, j, i.e. `w = 0`. Two independent implementations agree (`hess5.py`; the other agent's `b_hess5.py` with classes `[A F_ij]`; msolve/Singular on the 264 quadrics give the ideal `(w)²`).

**2. Exact Farkas certificates for every fixed-denominator exclusion.** A modular inconsistency `rank_p[M|b] = rank_p M + 1` proves inconsistency over ℚ only if `rank_p M = rank_ℚ M`; a single prime does not certify that. For each excluded `D` we therefore attach an exact rational vector `y` (supported on rank+1 rows of the character-sector system) with `yᵀM = 0`, `yᵀb ≠ 0`, verified in exact arithmetic — a prime-free proof over ℂ. Covered so far (100 named denominators, 48 degree-6 and 152 degree-7 sector elements/points, 35 cone elements; 335 certificates): `F_i`, `F_i²`, `B6`, `B6²`, `G_rad,i`, `G_rad,i²`, all 36 `Z_jF_i`, all 21 `F_iF_j`, `K10`, the two monomials, the pure-power / coupling / block / pair-Fermat / edge / single-coupling sextics and their squares, all 15 Fermat 4-cones and 20 Fermat 3-cones (cubic, quartic) and their squares, and every non-Jacobian E1-kernel element of degree 6 in every character sector. Certificates: `wave2-excl/certs/*.json` (row monomials, rational entries typically 100–2300 bits, up to ~15000 bits for a few sector elements, SHA-256), replay with `w2lib.cert_from_file`.

**3. Degree 6: every semi-invariant denominator is excluded.** `K_6 = {D : AD ∈ J}` has dimension 62 (36 = `J_6`, 26 non-Jacobian classes) over 46 characters of the order-72 diagonal group. Full table: `wave2-excl/sectors/table_d6.md` (46 sectors: 26 contain only a Jacobian product `z_jF_i`, 9 contain a single non-Jacobian class, 10 nontrivial sectors have `dim K ≥ 2`, plus the trivial sector). For a denominator of a single character κ the whole family `K_6^κ` is handled at once: in the ten sectors with `dim K_6^κ ≥ 2` and κ ≠ 1 the Hessian relaxation (`[H(D)] = 0` in `R_18`, a system of quadrics in the family coordinates) has **no projective solution** — computed modulo p = 10⁹+7 and, independently, exactly over ℚ (E1 kernel, Koszul-unique E1 solutions, normal forms modulo `J_18` with exact rational linear algebra; 6–9 quadrics per sector, Singular over ℚ: affine dim 0); the 35 one-dimensional sectors are single elements, each with an exact Farkas certificate. The trivial character `D = Σ a_i z_iF_i` is special: using `[A z_iF_i] = 0` the relaxation reduces to three explicit quadrics `(a·e(m))² = 6 Σ_i e_i(m) a_i²` (one per coupling monomial `m`), an irreducible degree-8 surface `V₀ ⊂ P⁵` through the Euler point `a = (1,…,1)`, `D = 6F`. On `V₀` we use the Koszul-parametrized form of the certificate equation (`N = Aw + K/2`, `K = α·∇F`): a certificate exists iff `H(D) + D div K − K·∇D = 2F²Q` is solvable in `(α, Q)` — a 467 × 278 sector system `M(a)x = b(a)`, linear in `a` with quadratic right-hand side. Polynomial Farkas vectors `y(a) = Σ a_k y_k` with `y(a)ᵀM(a) ≡ 0` identically in `a` (a 155-dimensional space) give cubic forms `φ_y(a) = y(a)ᵀb(a)`; we lifted 60 of them to exact rational vectors (verified exactly, entries ≤ 645 bits) and Singular over ℚ shows that the common zero locus of these 60 cubics in `P⁵` is the single Euler point — so every `a ≠ (1,…,1)` admits no certificate, by exact arithmetic, and the Euler point `D = F` is inconsistent (it would force `A ∈ J`; also an exact Farkas certificate) and illegal. **Conclusion: no degree-6 denominator of a single character, rigorously over ℂ.**

**4. Degree 6, denominators mixing several characters.** Restricting the 426 relaxation quadrics to the variables of two sectors (all 1035 pairs): the 990 pairs of nontrivial characters give an empty projective variety, and for the 45 pairs containing the trivial character the variety has no point with a nontrivial coordinate nonzero (saturation). The same holds for all 15180 unions of three characters (14190 nontrivial unions empty; 990 unions with the trivial character contained in `V₀`). So a degree-6 denominator needs at least **four** nonzero character components. Unions of four characters and the full 62-variable leading ideal (msolve) are being computed; until they finish, `deg D = 6` remains possible only for such ≥4-component denominators, and the rigorous statement is `deg D ≥ 6`.

**5. Degrees 7–12.** A structural remark first: `R_k = 0` for `k > 24`, so the relaxation `[H(D)] = 0 ∈ R_{2d+6}` is vacuous for `deg D ≥ 10`, a single socle condition at `d = 9`, and 21 quadrics at `d = 8`; and E1 is weak there (`Ann_8 = 825` of 951). So whole-family exclusions by this method end at `d = 7`; beyond that only fixed-denominator tests of individual structured denominators (with Farkas certificates) are available. At `d = 7` (23 of 72 character sectors analysed so far): `dim K_7^κ` = 6–12 per sector (total 180, of which 42 Jacobian), the sector relaxation (1–3 quadrics) leaves families of affine dimension 3–11, so no whole-sector exclusion; every non-Jacobian basis element and every random family point tested (246 so far) has an exact Farkas certificate. Coordinate-cone sub-families (forms in 3 or 4 of the variables, the shape favoured by the (3,1)-support heuristic) are enumerated per sector with their E1-kernel dimensions for `d = 8…12` (`sectors.py --cones`); at `d = 8` they are 1–6-dimensional per sector and subset — a finite list that the fixed-denominator solver can sweep. Certificates so far: 430 (101 named denominators, 48 degree-6 sector elements/points, 246 degree-7, 35 cone elements).
