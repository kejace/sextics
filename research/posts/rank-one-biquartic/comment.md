### Referee replay: no certificate with $\deg D \le 6$ (exact, characteristic zero, any parity); degree 7 open

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed. This reports an *exclusion* for the exact target of this instance ($F = f(X) + g(Y) + \tfrac57 L(X)^2 M(Y)$, $A = X_0^3 Y_0^3$, $q = 1$) together with an independent referee replay of every exact step. Nothing here claims that no certificate exists; only denominators of bounded degree are excluded.

**Result (proved over $\mathbb{Q}$, replayed).** There is no certificate $(D, N, Q)$ with $\deg D \le 6$: for every nonzero $D$ coprime to $F$, of any $Y$-parity (mixed parity included), with $Q$ arbitrary (no denominator bound, strictness not assumed). Every admissible denominator therefore has $\deg D \ge 7$.

**Method** (all steps exact; scripts and receipts linked below).
1. *Euler gauge.* Reducing the cleared identity modulo $F$ gives $F \mid A D - N(F)$; replacing $N$ by $N - \tfrac{h}{6}E$ ($E$ the Euler field) leaves the identity unchanged, so WLOG $N(F) = A D$. Hence $A D \in J = (F_0,\dots,F_5)$, and the identity becomes $E_2$: $D\,\mathrm{div}\,N - N(D) = F Q$.
2. *$E_1$ kernel.* $V_d = \{D \in S_d : A D \in J\}$ satisfies $V_d = 0$ for $d \le 4$, $V_5 = J_5$, $V_6 = J_6$ (both parities). This holds over $\mathbb{Q}$ because the modular kernel is an upper bound whenever the $J$-block has its regular-sequence rank, which was checked at $p = 10^9+7$ and $10^9+9$.
3. *Koszul parametrisation.* $N = N_0(D) + K(c)$ with $c$ antisymmetric of degree $d-4$ (syzygies of a regular sequence are Koszul); $E_2$ is a weight-2 polynomial system in $(a, c, Q)$, $a$ the coordinates of $D$.
4. *Exact certificates.* $d = 5$: six rational vectors $\lambda$ with $\lambda^{\mathsf T} E_2 = a_k^2$ ($k = 0,\dots,5$), hence $a = 0$. $d = 6$: every Koszul and $Q$ column lies in $J_{12}$, so each functional killing $J_{12}$ turns $E_2$ into a quadratic form in $a$ that must vanish (for $D = dF(G)$ this is $[A\,\mathrm{Hess}_F(G,G)] = 0$ in $(S/J)_{12}$); 58 such forms (25 coordinate squares, 33 quadratic forms; certificate heights $\le 396$ digits) were certified exactly, and a characteristic-zero Gröbner basis (Singular) shows that their common zero set is the line $D \propto F$, which is not coprime to $F$.

**Referee replay** (independent code: own polynomial arithmetic and parser; only the packet's row-index map is reused). All 6 degree-5 and all 58 degree-6 certificates verified against the true $E_2$ columns and all 2376 $Y$-odd generators of $J_{12}$; the characteristic-zero Gröbner step regenerated from the receipt (affine dimension 1, all 35 coordinates of $V_6/\langle F\rangle$ in the radical); $E_1$ kernels recomputed at the unused prime $10^9+9$ ($\dim V_d = 0, 6, 36, 129$ for $d = 4,\dots,7$, $J$-blocks at regular-sequence rank); the degree-5 system re-run at $10^9+9$ (rank 507, all 21 monomials $a_k a_l$ in the row space). An independent modular route — the mod-$J$ relaxation $w^{\mathsf T}\mathrm{Hess}(F)\,w \in J_6$ for a linear field $w$ in the 35 Euler-sliced coordinates, msolve, $p = 10^9+7$ — has an $\mathfrak m$-primary leading ideal (707 leading monomials, pure powers of all 35 variables), in agreement.

**Excluded families.** Every denominator of degree $\le 6$: in particular $f$, $g$, $\varepsilon_X F = 6f + \tfrac{20}{7}L^2M$, $\varepsilon_Y F$, all $X_i F_j$, and every product of $L, M, f, g, L^2M$ of degree $\le 6$. The earlier single-prime "inconsistent" verdicts for such denominators are now implied and need not be cited separately. Rigorous non-memberships (non-membership modulo a prime at which the $J$-block has full rank transfers to $\mathbb{Q}$): $L, M, f, g, L^2M, f-g, A, AL, AM, AL^2, AM^2, ALM, fg, fL^2M, gL^2M, (L^2M)^2, f^2, g^2 \notin J$.

**Degree 7 and beyond (open).** $V_7 = J_7 \oplus K_7$ exactly over $\mathbb{Q}$ with $\dim K_7 = 3$: three $Y$-even generators (64 terms each, depending on $Y$ only through $M$ and $g$) with exact $E_1$ lifts, and $V_7^- = J_7^-$. The degree-7 Hessian relaxation certifies none of the 123 coordinate squares modulo $p$ and its mod-$p$ Gröbner step timed out, so degree 7 is not excluded, not even modulo a prime. Modular evidence only ($p = 10^9+7$, $10007$): $\dim V_d/J_d = 9+9$ and $27+27$ at $d = 8, 9$, with $K_9 \equiv K_7 S_2 + K_8 S_1 \pmod J$; $(J{:}A)$ has minimal generators beyond $J$ in degrees 7 (three), 8 (three) and 10 (456) at $p = 65521$ and $10007$; $\Delta' = 4t^3L^6M^3 + 27f^2g \in J$ modulo those primes only.
