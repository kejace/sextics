### Wave-2 referee pass: every certificate for this instance has $\deg D \ge 6$ (exact), plus exact degree-6 exclusions

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed.

**Setting.** $F = H(X_0,X_1,Y_0,Y_1) + B(X_2,Y_2)$, $q = 1$, $A = X_0^3Y_0^3$. A certificate with denominator $D$ (coprime to $F$) satisfies $A D^2 - D\,N(F) + F\,(D\operatorname{div}N - N(D)) = F^2 Q$; reducing modulo $F$ gives (E1) $N(F) = AD + mF$, i.e. $[A][D] = 0$ in $R(F) = S/J(F)$, and the rest is (E2). Everything below was computed by an agent session (packet linked at the end) and then independently replayed by a referee pass with separate code (exact over $\mathbb{Q}$ with python-flint, or modulo the fresh prime $10^9+9$); "exact" means field-independent linear algebra, so the statements hold over $\mathbb{C}$.

**1. E1 structure (exact).** Since the variables split, $R(F) = R(H)\otimes R(B)$ and $\ker([A]\colon R(F)_d \to R(F)_{d+6}) = \bigoplus_k \ker(a\cdot\colon R(H)_{d-k}\to R(H)_{d-k+6})\otimes R(B)_k$ with $a = X_0^3Y_0^3$. The core kernels modulo $J(H)_k$, by $(Y_0,Y_1)$-parity (even/odd), are $k\le 5$: $0/0$; $6$: $20/13$; $7$: $30/30$; $8$: $41/34$; $9$: $38/38$; $10$: $36/31$; $11$: $26/26$; $12$: $19/16$ (exact: $a\cdot$ is surjective for $k \ge 5$, so $\dim\ker = \dim R(H)_k - \dim R(H)_{k+6}$; the Hilbert functions of $R(H)$ and $R(B)$ are the complete-intersection ones over $\mathbb{Q}$). Hence **no E1-admissible denominator of degree $\le 4$ exists, in any parity**, and **every E1-admissible denominator of degree 5 is a polar $F_v = v\cdot\nabla F$, $v\in\mathbb{C}^6$.** This makes the earlier single-prime, $Y$-even screens (dossier, $p = 2^{31}-1$) exact and parity-free.

**2. Degree 5 is excluded over $\mathbb{C}$ (exact).** For $D = F_v$, E1 forces $N = Av + \tfrac{m}{6}z + K$ with $K$ Koszul (linear coefficients), and E2 reduces to $F_vA_v - A\,F_{vv} + F_v\operatorname{div}K - K(F_v) = FQ$ ($m$ cancels; $F_{vv} = v^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{Hess}(F)\,v$). Replacing $v\otimes v$ and $v\otimes\ell$ by free tensors gives a linear system (2078 equations, $21+540+126$ unknowns, rank 435 over $\mathbb{Q}$) whose kernel projects onto an 8-dimensional $W\subset\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbb{C}^6$ spanned by the mixed core$\times$binary coordinates $e_ae_b$ ($a\in\{X_0,X_1,Y_0,Y_1\}$, $b\in\{X_2,Y_2\}$). Every $v_i^2$ vanishes on $W$, so $W$ contains no rank-one tensor, so no certificate has $\deg D = 5$. (Control: for a target in $J(F)$ the same code returns $W = \operatorname{Sym}^2$.) **Therefore $\deg D\ge 6$ for every certificate of this instance.** By the prescribed-support theorem of the book (ch. 1), at $q = 1$ this is a statement about supports: no certificate has poles confined to a hypersurface $V(D_{\mathrm{red}})$ with $\deg D_{\mathrm{red}}\le 5$.

**3. Degree 6 (partial).** E1-admissible sextics are $D = F_w + c$ with $w\in(S_1)^6$ (36 parameters) and $c$ in the 33-dimensional core-only kernel.
* *Jacobian type, $G$-eigenvectors (exact).* Under the diagonal symmetry group $G$ (order 24, generated by $(X_2,Y_2)\mapsto\zeta_6(X_2,Y_2)$, $X_2\mapsto -X_2$, $(Y_0,Y_1)\mapsto -(Y_0,Y_1)$) the eigenvector members of the Jacobian family fall into 11 character classes ($10 + 8 + 9\times 2$ parameters; the 10-parameter class contains all weighted Euler polars $\sum\lambda_a z_aF_a$). The sector-restricted Hessian relaxation of each class has no rank-one point, except the Euler line $D = 6\lambda F$ in the 10-parameter class (not coprime to $F$). **No $G$-eigenvector Jacobian-type sextic admits a certificate.** Non-eigenvector members ($w$ mixing characters) are not covered.
* *Fixed rational $D$, rigorous over $\mathbb{C}$ by the nullity argument* (inconsistent modulo $p$, and the exact rational nullity $n_m + \dim\{K\ \text{Koszul}: D\operatorname{div}K - K(D)\in F\cdot S\}$ equals the modular nullity): $B(X_2,Y_2)$ and $H(X_0,X_1,Y_0,Y_1)$ (both in $J(F)$; rank 2298 of 2602 unknowns, nullity 304); and every exact rational representative of the core-only E1 kernels of degrees 6, 7, 8 ($20+13$, $30+30$, $41+34$ representatives, plus 6 random same-parity combinations per degree), each tested in the character-sector-restricted full 6-variable system (lossless for these $G$-eigenvectors): all 186 inconsistent over $\mathbb{C}$. Mixed-parity kernel elements and the general $69$-parameter sextic family remain untested.

**4. Not done / open.** The sum-principle test of products $c(x)\,b(X_2,Y_2)$, generic E1-admissible members of degrees 7–10, and the core-kernel sweep for degrees 9–12 were planned but did not run (compute capacity); no result is claimed for them. Nothing here is a nonexistence statement: the open question remains whether some denominator of degree $\ge 6$ outside the tested families, or a correspondence supplying the missing Prym support identified by `benediamond` above, serves $A$.
