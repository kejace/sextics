## Exclusion results, wave 2: every certificate denominator has degree $\ge 7$; degrees $7$–$9$ are Jacobian denominators with a quadratic Hessian condition

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed. Nothing below claims that no certificate exists.

**Setup.** $q=1$, $A=X_0^3Y_0^3$, $F$ the 236-term $\tau$-even sextic, $J=J(F)$, $R=S/J$ (Gorenstein, socle degree 24), $\tau(X,Y)=(X,-Y)$. Writing the cleared identity $E=F^2Q$ in the book's normalized form gives E1: $N(F)\equiv AD \pmod F$, hence $A\cdot D\in J$, and E2: $D\,\mathrm{div}N-N(D)\equiv 0 \pmod F$. The $\tau$-reduction $D\mapsto D\cdot\tau D$ only shows that *some* certificate has an even denominator, so all degree bounds below cover $D$ of pure and of mixed $Y$-parity. "Rigorous" means proved over $\mathbb{Q}$ (hence $\mathbb{C}$); modular computations carry the rank-transfer argument stated with them. Prior geometry in this thread is `benediamond`'s (plane rigidity, residual quintics, the $(2,2,4)$ net fibres); it is used only as context.

**1. E1 kernel (rigorous).** For $d\le 9$ and both parities, $\{D\in S_d : AD\in J\}=J_d$, i.e. $\mathrm{Ann}_R(A)_d=0$. Rank argument: $\dim_{\mathbb Q}\ker\le n_D-\mathrm{rank}_p[A\cdot m\,|\,J_{d+6}]+\dim_{\mathbb Q}J_{d+6}$, and in every row $\mathrm{rank}_p J_{d+6}$ equals the exact Koszul value from the parity-refined Hilbert series $(1-t^5)^3(1-st^5)^3/((1-t)^3(1-st)^3)$. Four primes ($2^{31}-1$, $2147483629$, $2147483587$, $10^9+9$), three code paths (nullspace, normal forms, plain ranks). At $d=10$ the kernel modulo $J$ is exactly the forced $294$ (even) / $261$ (odd) $=\dim R_{10}^\pm-\dim R_{8}^\pm$.

**2. Reduction (exact).** Hence for $d\le 9$ every denominator is $D=w(F)=\sum_i w_i\,\partial_iF$ with $w_i\in S_{d-5}$, coprime to $F$ iff $w\notin\{h\cdot\mathrm{Euler}\}$. Because the syzygies of $(\partial_iF)$ are Koszul, a certificate exists iff the Koszul-reduced system $[D\,\mathrm{div}N_0-N_0(D)]+\sum_{i<j}[D(F_j\partial_ic_{ij}-F_i\partial_jc_{ij})-c_{ij}(F_jD_i-F_iD_j)]=FQ$, $N_0=Aw$, $c_{ij}\in S_{d-4}$, is consistent (the $\mu\cdot$Euler freedom cancels identically). Reducing modulo $J$ gives the necessary **Hessian condition** $A\cdot\mathrm{Hess}_F(w,w)\in J_{2d}$, for $d\le 7$ equivalently $\mathrm{Hess}_F(w,w)\in J_{2d-6}$; it depends only on $w$ modulo $h\cdot$Euler.

**3. Degrees $\le 6$ are excluded for $D$ of any parity (rigorous).** $d\le 4$: $J_d=0$. $d=5$: $w$ is constant and $\mathrm{Hess}_F(w,w)\in J_4=0$; the $21$ second partials $F_{ij}$ are linearly independent over $\mathbb{Q}$ (exact rank 21), so $w=0$. $d=6$: $w$ linear; the ideal of quadrics $\mathrm{NF}_J\,\mathrm{Hess}_F(w,w)$ in the gauge-fixed coordinates is $\mathfrak m$-primary: Hilbert functions $[1,17,18,2,0]$ (even, 17 variables), $[1,18,18,2,0]$ (odd, 18), and for mixed $w=w_++w_-$ (35 variables, 405 independent quadrics) $[1,35,225,400,225,36,1,0]$, colength $923$. Computed with msolve (`-g 1`) at $2147483587$, $2^{31}-1$, $10^9+7$ and $10^9+9$, Singular (`dim 0`, `vdim`) as a second engine. Transfer: with $\mathrm{rank}_pJ_6=18$ exact, the mod-$p$ quadrics are the reduction of the rational ones, and a full-rank Macaulay matrix mod $p$ stays full rank over $\mathbb{Q}$, so the only solution over $\mathbb{C}$ is $w\in\mathbb{C}\cdot$Euler, i.e. $D=6cF$. Exact cross-checks: the six partials $\partial F/\partial Z_i$ are inconsistent over $\mathbb{Q}$ (Koszul-reduced system $3003\times 216$, rank $210$ vs $211$).

**4. Degree 7.** Pure parity: $483$ independent quadrics in $60$ variables per parity; the full Gröbner computation (degree of regularity 4, $\gtrsim 40$ GB) was OOM-killed on the shared box (rc 137 after 5864 s at ~40 GB) and reruns are in progress — **open**. Mixed parity ($951$ quadrics, $120$ variables): not attempted. Excluded rigorously (both parities, same transfer): gradient fields $w=\nabla G$, $G$ cubic ($D=\nabla F\cdot\nabla G$; linearization rank $406=\dim\mathrm{Sym}^2$); arrangement-preserving fields $\sum h_iR_i+\sum g_iE_i$ (colength 29); fields supported on the $X$- or the $Y$-components (708, 439); coefficients in $\mathbb{C}[X]$ (203) or $\mathbb{C}[X_0,Y_0]$ (13). Screened only (single prime, no rational dual): 140 individual fields (random, arrangement, Hamiltonian pairs, gradient) — all inconsistent with the same signature (rank 996, augmented 997, 1056 unknowns).

**5. Degree 8.** Condition $\mathrm{Hess}_F(w,w)\in\mathrm{Ann}_R(A)_{10}^++J_{10}^+$ ($468$ quadrics in $156/159$ variables, degree of regularity $\ge 8$ by the Hilbert-series bound): out of Gröbner reach. Excluded rigorously: Hamiltonian fields $w=C\nabla G$ ($C$ constant antisymmetric) for $G\in\{K_i,H_i,\mathrm{diag}_i,Q_iQ_j,Q_i^2\}$ and random quartics (linearization full rank), coefficients in $\mathbb{C}[X_0,Y_0]$ (28) or $\mathbb{C}[X_0,X_1,X_2]$ (4006), and all single-component fields $h\,\partial_{Z_i}$ ($D=hF_i$, colength 740 in all 12 cases). Degree 9: the relaxation has only $\sim 208$ quadrics in $\sim 350$ variables (positive-dimensional); no complete route and no results yet.

**6. Sum-principle bookkeeping (rigorous, given the book).** For 36 natural factors — $Q_i$, $Q_i\pm Q_j$, $\sum Q_i$, $X_iY_i$, $X_i^2-Y_i^2$, $X_0^2$, $X_0X_1$, $X_0Y_1$; $K_i$, $H_i$, $\mathrm{diag}_i$, $Q_i^2$, $Q_iQ_j$; $Q_0Q_1Q_2$, Fermat — $\mathrm{Ann}_R(D)_6^{\mathrm{odd}}=\ker(\cdot D: R_6^-\to R_{6+\deg D})=0$ (rank argument as in 1, no-drop ranks recorded at $10^9+9$), so each factor serves only classes in $J$. By the prescribed-support theorem (Ch. 1: at $q=1$ poles in $V(D)$ iff denominator $D_{\mathrm{red}}$) and the Gysin description of divisor support (Ch. 3), $\mathrm{served}(D_1D_2)=\mathrm{served}(D_1)+\mathrm{served}(D_2) \bmod J$; hence no product of these factors, e.g. $g_5(Q_0,Q_1,Q_2)$ or $Q_0Q_1Q_2\cdot G_4$, serves $A$ (recall $A\notin J$).

**Open.** Degree 7 in full (pure parity: pending Gröbner run; mixed: untouched); degrees 8–9 beyond the listed subfamilies; all $d\ge 10$, where non-Jacobian E1-admissible denominators form the 294/261-dimensional $\mathrm{Ann}_R(A)_{10}$. None of this bears on existence at higher degree.
