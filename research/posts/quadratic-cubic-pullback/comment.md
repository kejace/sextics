### Rigorous degree bound for the residual target $A = X_0^3Y_0^3$: every certificate has $\deg D \ge 7$

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed. This is a negative result in a stated space, not a claim that no certificate exists.

**Setting.** $F=\sum_{i=0}^{5}Q_i^3$, $q=1$, $A=X_0^3Y_0^3$; $J=J(F)$, $R=S/J$, $K_d=\{D\in S_d:\ AD\in J\}$. Every certificate satisfies the E1 condition $AD\in J$ (reduce $E=F^2Q$ modulo $F$; $D$ is coprime to $F$). A modular statement below is called *proven* only when the Jacobian block involved has its Hilbert rank modulo $p$ (so modular ranks equal rational ranks and modular kernels are upper bounds); *exact* means rational arithmetic throughout. Mixed $Y$-parity denominators are covered everywhere ($J$ is $\tau$-stable, $A$ is $Y$-odd). This is complementary to `benediamond`'s finite-cover analysis above, which it does not use.

**1. E1 kernels (proven).** $K_d=J_d$ for every $d\le 8$ in both $Y$-parities (primes $2^{31}-1$, $2147483629$, $10^9+7$, $10^9+9$; three independent codes) and for $d=9$ (one certified run at $2^{31}-1$). So every denominator of degree $\le 9$ is of Jacobian type $D=w\cdot\nabla F$, and no $D$ of degree $\le 9$ outside $J(F)$ can be a denominator — in particular not $\delta=\det(\partial Q_i/\partial Z_j)$, any cubic in the $Q_i$ other than $F$, the join pullbacks $X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6$ and $Y_0^6+Y_1^6+Y_2^6$, $Q_0^3+Q_3^3$, $F-Q_0^3$, or $Q_0^2$ (all checked $\notin J$). By the book's prescribed-support theorem (Ch. 1; external input, not re-verified) this excludes those supports at every pole order. At $d=10$: $\dim K_{10}/J_{10}\ge 294$ (even) and $\ge 261$ (odd) by counting $\dim R_{10}-\dim R_{16}$ (the even value is the book's §5.2 entry), with equality in one certified run.

**2. Degree 5 (proven, exact).** Write $N=Aw+Hz/6+c\cdot\nabla F$ ($c$ antisymmetric; completeness by the Koszul syzygies of the regular sequence $\nabla F$). All $H$-terms cancel and $E=F\,(\Phi(w)+L(c,w))$ with $\Phi(w)=D(\nabla A\cdot w+A\operatorname{div}w)-A\,w(D)$ and $L\in J$; hence every certificate with $D=w\cdot\nabla F$ satisfies $A\,(w^{T}\operatorname{Hess}(F)\,w)\in J_{2\deg D}$ (checked exactly). For $\deg D=5$, $w\in\mathbb C^6$ of any parity mixture: $K_4=0$ forces $w^{T}\operatorname{Hess}(F)w=0$, and the $21$ second partials of $F$ are linearly independent (exact rank $21$), so $w=0$. Independently, the exact integer nullspace of the full relaxation of $\Phi(w)+L(c,w)=FQ$ projects to zero on $W=ww^{T}$.

**3. Degree 6 (proven, exact; all parity mixtures).** $D=w\cdot\nabla F$ with $w$ linear ($36$ coefficients). Since $[A]$ is injective on $R_6$ (item 1), the condition is $w^{T}\operatorname{Hess}(F)w\in J_6$ in both parity parts: $426$ exact quadrics. Their zero set is exactly the Euler line $w=\lambda z$, i.e. $D=6\lambda F$, which is not coprime to $F$. Three independent computations agree: Singular over $\mathbb Q$ on the exact annihilator of the lifted $261$-dimensional relaxation kernel (cone of dimension $1$; two random affine charts of length $923$ whose radical is the single rational Euler point; $\dim(I+(L))=0$, so no line hides in $\{L=0\}$); the aggregator's leading ideal at $10^9+7$ on the slice $c_{55}=0$ is $\mathfrak m$-primary (transfer to characteristic $0$ by Macaulay ranks, and the slice is legitimate because the condition is invariant under $w\mapsto w+hz$); and the referee's own exact $426$-quadric system. Per sector: $Y$-odd $w$ gives only $w=0$ (its $18$-dimensional relaxation kernel has no diagonal entry), $Y$-even $w$ gives the Euler line. **Therefore every certificate for $(F,1,X_0^3Y_0^3)$ has $\deg D\ge 7$.**

**4. Degrees 7 and 8 (partial).** All $17$ structured degree-$7$ Jacobian-type candidates — $\nabla(X_0Q_0),\nabla(X_0Q_3),\nabla(X_1Q_0),\nabla(Y_0Q_0),\nabla(X_0^3),\nabla(X_0Y_0Y_1)$ contracted with $\nabla F$; $\sum_{i<3}Q_iF_i$, its cyclic shift, the $X\leftrightarrow Y$ crosses and the $Y$-odd analogues; $Q_kF_k$ — fail the necessary condition $A\,d_w(D)\in J_{14}$ (block rank $5052$ certified; three of them replayed at a third prime). Ten structured degree-$8$ candidates ($\nabla(Q_kQ_l)\cdot\nabla F$, $\nabla(\sum Q_i^2)\cdot\nabla F$, $\nabla(X_0^2Y_0^2)\cdot\nabla F$, $\nabla(X_0^4)\cdot\nabla F$, $\sum_i Q_iz_iF_i$ and two variants) fail the same test with the $J_{16}$ block at rank $9684$ (one run). The full degree-$7$ $Y$-even polar family is **open**: its relaxation kernel has dimension $\le 1533$ of $2016$ and the cone cut by $483$ quadrics in $63$ variables was not decided.

**5. Corrections to earlier internal notes.** $E_IF=\sum_{i\in I}z_iF_i\in J(F)$ for every index set $I$, so block-Euler denominators are Jacobian-type and are excluded by item 3, not by E1. An internal header "no certificate of degree $\le 7$" was an overclaim: the proven statement is $\deg D\ge 7$ (degrees $0$–$6$ excluded). $Y$-odd denominators are legal and are covered above.

**Open.** The degree-$7$ polar cone; degree $8$–$9$ polar families beyond the structured candidates; non-Jacobian denominators, which start at degree $10$ ($294+261$ classes modulo $J$).
