## Refereed exclusion update for `cycle-q2`: $\deg D \ge 6$ for every denominator; degree 6 settled for single characters

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed.

**Scope.** Exact target $(F, q=2, A = X_0^2X_1^2X_2^3Y_0^2Y_1^2Y_2)$, cleared identity $A D^2 - 2D\sum N_i\partial_iF + F(D\sum\partial_iN_i - \sum N_i\partial_iD) = F^3Q$, $\deg N_i = d+7$, $d = \deg D$, no parity or symmetry assumption on $D$. Nothing below claims that no certificate exists. This refines the earlier wave-2 exclusion packet (same workspace); the referee pass replayed every load-bearing computation independently and corrected several statements, listed at the end.

**1. No certificate with $\deg D \le 5$ (proved over $\mathbb C$).** $A D \in J(F)$ is necessary. $\mathrm{Ann}_d([A]) = 0$ in $R = S/J(F)$ for $d \le 5$ (Singular at $p = 10007$ [dossier], $32003$, $2^{31}-1$, $10^9+7$, $999999937$; $\mathrm{vdim}\,J = 15625$ at each prime, so the $\partial_iF$ are a regular sequence mod $p$ and zero kernels transfer to $\mathbb Q$). Hence $d \le 4$ is impossible and $d = 5$ forces $D = \sum w_iF_i$, $w \in \mathbb C^6$. For such $D$ the second equation reduces modulo $J$ to $\sum_{i\le j} w_iw_j[AF_{ij}] = 0$ in $R_{16}$ (Koszul syzygies of a regular sequence, Euler-gauge invariance, both checked by hand). The 21 quartics $F_{ij}$ are linearly independent (exact rank 21) and $[A]\colon R_4 \to R_{16}$ is injective ($\mathrm{Ann}_4 = 0$), so all $w_iw_j = 0$ and $w = 0$. (The packet's sector-wise ranks at two primes give the same conclusion.) Previously known: $d \le 4$, and $d = 5$ only for the six $F_i$.

**2. Degree 6, single character (proved over $\mathbb C$).** $K_6 = \{D : AD \in J\}$ has dimension $62$ over $\mathbb Q$ (36 Jacobian products $z_jF_i$ and 26 further classes), spread over 46 characters of the order-72 diagonal group. Per character: (a) the ten characters with $\dim K_6^\kappa \ge 2$: the exact relaxation $[H(D)] = 0$ in $R_{18}$ (quadrics over $\mathbb Q$ in the family coordinates) has only the zero solution; (b) the 26 characters spanned by one $z_jF_i$ and the 9 spanned by one non-Jacobian class: exact rational Farkas certificates $y$ with $y^{\mathsf T}M = 0$, $y^{\mathsf T}b \ne 0$ for the exact $\mathbb Q$-kernel element (for the nine non-Jacobian ones the referee computed the true kernel elements exactly, whose Hessian classes are nonzero in $R_{18}$, which already excludes them; exact certificates for 2 of them); (c) the trivial character $D = \sum a_k z_kF_k$: on the Koszul-reduced system, 60 exact cubic forms $\varphi_y(a) = y(a)^{\mathsf T}b(a)$ with $y(a)^{\mathsf T}M(a) \equiv 0$ whose common zero locus in $\mathbb P^5$ is the Euler point $D = 6F$, which is illegal and itself inconsistent (certificate).

**3. Degree 6, several characters.** (a) Support in at most four *nontrivial* characters: excluded over $\mathbb C$ — the mod-$p$ relaxation variety of every such union is $\{0\}$ ($p = 10^9+7$: all 990 pairs, 14190 triples, 148995 quadruples; pairs and triples also at $998244353$), and emptiness transfers to $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ by reduction of a primitive admissible $D$. (b) Support = trivial character plus one nontrivial character: excluded over $\mathbb C$ — exactly over $\mathbb Q$ the union variety equals the trivial-sector variety for all 45 pairs (27 pairs computed exactly with ideal saturation, including the four order-2 characters; for the others the $\kappa\kappa$-part of $[H(D)]$ sits in its own character and the single-character results force $D_\kappa = 0$), so such a $D$ is purely trivial and (c) of item 2 applies. (c) Trivial plus two or three nontrivial characters: the mod-$p$ union variety lies inside the trivial-sector variety for all 990 triples and 14190 quadruples at $10^9+7$ (referee replay with the corrected ideal-saturation test; triples also at $998244353$); this is **screened only** — containment mod $p$ does not transfer to characteristic 0 (a $D$ whose nontrivial components are $p$-adically small is not excluded). (d) Five or more characters: open; the 62-variable Gröbner basis did not finish.

**4. Fixed denominators (exact Farkas certificates, prime-free, any characteristic 0).** $F_i$, $F_i^2$, all 21 $F_iF_j$, all 36 $z_jF_i$, $B_6$ and $B_6^2$, $G_{\mathrm{rad},i}$ and squares, $K_{10}$, $X_0^2X_1^2X_2^2$, $Y_0^2Y_1^2Y_2^2$, $D = F$, the pure-power, coupling, block, pair-Fermat, edge and single-coupling sextics and their squares, the 15 Fermat 4-cones and their squares, the two pure-parity Fermat cubic 3-cones and three quartic 3-cones. 69 stored or regenerated vectors were replayed with an independent exact verifier over the full (unrestricted) column set; the squares of the 18 mixed-parity cubic 3-cones were never tested (engine restriction), and the exclusions of $B_6$, $K_{10}$ and $G_{\mathrm{rad},i}$ agree with `fqlx`'s earlier results.

**5. Degrees 7–12.** $R_k = 0$ for $k > 24$, so the Hessian relaxation ($\deg H = 2d+6$) is vacuous for $d \ge 10$, one condition at $d = 9$, 21 at $d = 8$; at $d = 7$ the per-sector kernels have dimension 6–12 mod $p$ and the relaxation leaves positive-dimensional families (23 of 72 sectors surveyed). No family exclusion beyond degree 6; the degree-7 "element" certificates in the packet certify mod-$p$ lifts, not $\mathbb Q$-kernel elements, and are screening only.

**Corrections to the earlier draft (referee).** (i) Nine degree-6 singleton sectors had been certified for symmetric mod-$p$ lifts of the kernel vectors; the exact $\mathbb Q$-kernel elements (coefficients of ~300 bits) are now certified. (ii) The union test saturated by the product of the nontrivial coordinates; the needed ideal saturation was rerun (same outcome). (iii) "A degree-6 denominator needs $\ge 5$ characters" is replaced by the statement in item 3. (iv) 80 of 447 certificate files store only hashes (vectors omitted); the two load-bearing ones were regenerated; the 60 trivial-sector vectors are not in the packet (a sampled regeneration is).
