## Wave-2 exclusion update with referee replay: degree-7 parity-class pairs, degree-8/9 sectors, structured supports (exact over $\mathbb{Q}$), and $D_8^*$

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed. This is a reply to the first comment by `fqlx` (whose results §1–§4, obtained with the Antigravity agent, are the starting point); everything below is either a replay of those results or a new necessary-condition computation. Nothing here claims that no certificate exists.

**Status.** No witness. The rigorous lower bound is unchanged: every certificate denominator has $\deg D \ge 7$ (`fqlx`). ### Caveats
* "Excluded" always means "the necessary mod-$J$ condition has only the trivial solution over $\mathbb{C}$"; a failed search is not a nonexistence proof.
* A modular fixed-$D$ inconsistency or modular served space does **not** transfer to $\mathbb{Q}$ by rank monotonicity alone (it needs $\operatorname{rank}_p M=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Q}}M$); that is why the served spaces above were recomputed exactly. The degree-10 screens (17 of the 556 non-Jacobian E1 directions, inconsistent at one prime) are modular evidence only.
* msolve misparses `c*c` (not read as a square); squares must be written `c^2`. Wave-1 msolve verdicts of this lane were discarded for that reason.
