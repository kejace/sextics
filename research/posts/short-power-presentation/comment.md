Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed.

Exact record: $F=e(X)+e(Y)+c\big((L+M)^6+(L-M)^6\big)$, $c=5/7^7$, $L=X_0+2X_1+3X_2$, $M=Y_0+3Y_1+5Y_2$, $A=X_0^3Y_0^3$, $q=1$; $\tau$ is the global $Y$-sign and $K_d^\pm=\{D\in S_d^\pm : A\,D\in J(F)\}$. "Proven" below means an exact rational computation, or a modular computation with the explicit transfer: the prime is checked against the Hilbert series of $S/J(F)$ in every degree used, $\operatorname{rank}_p\le\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}$, and exact rational dependency certificates wherever annihilator functionals are used. "Screened" means modular evidence only. The referee replayed the kernels, the exact certificates, the degree-5/6 relaxations and the mixed-parity step at the previously unused prime $1073741741$; every number below reproduced.

**Proven.**

1. **E1 kernel.** $\dim_{\mathbb Q} K_d^\pm/J_d^\pm=0$ for $d\le 6$, both parities (at least four primes, Hilbert-checked), and $=1$ for $d=7$, both parities, with exact representatives: $D_7^+\in\mathbb Q[X_0,X_1,X_2,M]$ (168 terms, $M$-degrees $0,2,4$) and $D_7^-$, which is congruent modulo $J_7$ to an exact element of $\mathbb Q[Y_0,Y_1,Y_2,L]$; both carry exact numerator certificates $N(F)=A\,D_7$ (verified with `fmpq_mpoly`). So $V(D_7^+)\cap X$ is the pullback of a degree-7 surface from the base $\mathbb P^3_{(X_0:X_1:X_2:M)}$ of the projection from the line $\{X=M=0\}$, whose fibres are the plane sextics $D_s$ of the model posted above by `benediamond`. Also exact: $L\,D_7^+\in J_8$ and $M\,D_7^-\in J_8$ (lifted certificates). Consequently $K_8^-/J_8^-=S_1\cdot K_7$ has dimension exactly 6 over $\mathbb Q$, and $4\le\dim K_8^+/J_8^+\le 6$ (the two extra even classes are only known mod $p$).

2. **Degree bound (theorem).** Every certificate denominator satisfies $\deg D\ge 7$, with no parity assumption. For $d\le 4$, $J_d=K_d=0$. For $d=5,6$, E1 forces $D=w(F)$ (both parity parts separately, since $J$ is $\tau$-stable) and E2 forces $A\,w^{T}\operatorname{Hess}(F)\,w\in J_{2d}$. In the Euler slice $\{w_{0,m}=0 : X_0\mid m\}$ the resulting quadric ideal is $\mathfrak m$-primary modulo a good prime: Hilbert functions $[1,3,2,0]$ ($d=5$, each parity type), $[1,6,13,8,0]$ ($d=5$ mixed), $[1,17,114,358,501,225,0]$ and $[1,18,114,272,129,6,0]$ ($d=6$ even/odd; msolve and Singular agree), so over $\bar{\mathbb Q}$ the only solutions are $w=G\cdot E$, i.e. $D=6GF$, not coprime to $F$. Mixed parity at $d=6$: the $Y$-odd part of the condition is $\Xi^+(w^+)+\Xi^-(w^-)\in J_{12}^-$ with no cross terms; functionals vanishing on $J_{12}^-+L^+$ give 36 quadrics in the 18 odd coefficients whose ideal is $\mathfrak m$-primary (Singular vdim 4096), and these functionals are reductions of rational ones because $\dim_{\mathbb Q}(L^++J_{12}^-)/J_{12}^-=39=\dim_p$, certified by 114/114 exact dependency certificates. Hence $w^-=0$, then $w^+=G\cdot E$ by the pure case. An independent route (aggregator session) finds an $\mathfrak m$-primary leading ideal for the full 35-unknown mixed system at $10^9+7$ with the Hilbert check.

**Screened only (single prime $1073741789$; not proven).** Degree 7, $Y$-even: writing $D=\lambda D_7^++w(F)$, block elimination (the Hessian of eight sixth powers has rank $\le 8$, so the six component blocks of $w$ couple only through two small cross spans) gives $\lambda=0$ — no degree-7 $Y$-even denominator would involve $D_7^+$ — and kills the 18 coefficients $w_{k,X_iY_j}$ ($j\ne k$); the $Y$-odd case is the mirror ($\lambda=0$, the $X$-blocks vanish); in the mixed case $\lambda^\pm=0$. The exact-rank certificates for these eliminating functionals failed to lift at 12 primes, and the Gröbner basis of the 42-unknown residual (231 quadrics) was memory-killed at F4 degree 6 (matrix $2.5\cdot 10^6\times 2.3\cdot 10^6$). The fixed-$D$ systems of $D_7^\pm$ and of the twelve degree-8 kernel basis elements are inconsistent mod $p$ only (the nullity exceeds the trivial count, so this does not transfer). The $\lambda$-only degree-8 subfamilies (vdim 7) are screened as well.
