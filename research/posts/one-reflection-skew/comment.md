Items marked ✓ were replayed by a separate referee pass with independent code (exactly over $\mathbb Z$, or at the fresh primes 1000000007 / 998244353).

4. **Lemma (proved; the strict form of the five-variable reduction `fringewidth` stated in #54).** For $Y_2$-free $D$ a certificate exists iff some $Y_2$-free $N'$ of degree $d+1$ satisfies $N'(f)=AD$ and $D\,\mathrm{div}N'-N'(D)=0$ (no $Q$, no $F$). Proof: average over $\mu_6(Y_2)$, Euler-normalize, specialize $Y_2^6\to-f$ using $\partial_s$ of $E_1$ (giving $\tilde N(f)=AD+6f\tilde a$, $B_D(\tilde N)=5\tilde aD$), then $N'=\tilde N-\tilde aE'$. Checked line by line by two referee passes.

**Corrected / withdrawn.**
6. Degree-7 pure even classes 0010, 0100, 1000. Both lane relaxations (six-variable, and strict five-variable on the $Y_2$-free part) give, at two primes, Gröbner leading ideals with pure powers of all coordinates but one. An internal reading "the relaxed ideal is supported on one coordinate axis, which is excluded exactly" is **withdrawn**: a pure power in the leading ideal is not a pure power in the ideal; the correct consequence is Krull dimension 1, i.e. finitely many projective points (in the five-variable system the generators restrict to $-68036492\,l_{36}^2\neq0$ on that axis, so the axis is not even a solution). Rigorous statement: in each class the denominators satisfying the necessary relaxed quadrics form a **finite set of projective points** — for class 0100 directly over $\mathbb Q$ (Singular `std`: dimension 1, degree 67, in both relaxations), for 0010/1000 of length ≤ 495 / 419 by the mod-$p$ Hilbert functions. The points are not identified, so **this packet does not close these classes**; `fqlx`'s m-primary Gröbner computation on the base complement remains the only claimed closure and was not replayed here.
7. Open (unchanged from the thread): class 1110, mixtures of ≥2 even characters, every $d\ge8$. Pending from this packet: the 129-coordinate degree-7 leaf (1094 relaxed quadrics of rank 757, no coordinate killed; msolve/Singular not finished), the 31 remaining structured candidates. Earlier wave-1 five-variable screens from this account are single-prime evidence only.
