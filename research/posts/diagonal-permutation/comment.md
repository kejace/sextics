**Exclusion data for `diagonal-permutation` (wave 2): no certificate with $\deg D \le 6$ for any $D$; the degree 7–9 picture**

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed.

**Setup.** $q=1$; a certificate $(D,N,Q)$ satisfies $A D^2 - D\,N(F) + F\,(D\,\mathrm{div}N - N(D)) = F^2 Q$ with $D$ coprime to $F$. Reducing mod $F$ gives $N(F) = AD + FH$, so $AD \in J(F)$ (**E1**), and since the partials form a regular sequence, $N = u + K + \tfrac{H}{6}E$ with $u(F) = AD$, $K$ Koszul, $E$ Euler. Substituting, the $H$-terms cancel and the identity becomes $F\,(\Psi(D;u) + D\,\mathrm{div}K - K(D)) = F^2 Q$ with $\Psi(D;u) := D\,\mathrm{div}\,u - u(D)$. **Lemma.** $D\,\mathrm{div}K - K(D) \in J$ for every Koszul field, hence every certificate has $\Psi(D;u) \in J$ ("E2 modulo $J$"); $\Psi \bmod J$ does not depend on $u$ or on $D \bmod F\cdot S$, and for $D = w(F)$ one has $\Psi \equiv -A\, w^{\mathsf T}\mathrm{Hess}(F)\, w$. For Jacobian-type denominators the necessary condition is therefore a homogeneous quadratic system in the coefficients of $w$ (Euler multiples $gE$, i.e. $D = 6gF$, removed by a gauge).

**Proven** (modular kernels bound rational kernels from above; a zero-dimensional reduced Gröbner basis mod $p$ transfers to $\mathbb Q$ by the Hilbert-function argument; good reduction of $J$ in every degree used is recorded in the receipts; all statements hold over $\mathbb C$):

1. **$\deg D \ge 7$ for every homogeneous $D$ (Y-even, Y-odd or mixed).** (i) $d \le 4$: $\ker([A]\cdot : R_d \to R_{d+6}) = 0$ in both parities and $J_d = 0$. (ii) $d = 5,6,7$: $\ker[A] = 0$ in both parities, so every E1-admissible $D$ is $w(F)$ with $\deg w_i = d-5$. (iii) $d = 5$: $w$ constant and $w^{\mathsf T}\mathrm{Hess}(F)w = 0$ as a quartic; the 21 second partials $F_{ij}$ are linearly independent over $\mathbb Q$ (exact rank 21), so $w = 0$. (iv) $d = 6$: $w$ linear; the relaxation systems (Y-even 17 unknowns / 134 independent quadrics, Y-odd 18 / 153, mixed 35 / 404) have zero-dimensional reduced Gröbner bases with a pure power of every variable (msolve 0.6.5, confirmed by Singular 4.3.2, colengths 39 / 39 / 924); so the only degree-6 Jacobian denominators passing E2 mod $J$ are multiples of $F$. Primes: $10^9+7$ and $998244353$ for the kernels, $10^9+7$ for the Gröbner bases; the degree-6 system was built independently twice (lens and aggregator) and a referee pass rebuilt everything a third time with engine-free code at the fresh prime $999999937$ (same counts, same colengths, msolve and Singular).
2. **$d = 8$:** $\ker[A]$ on $R_8^+$ is exactly one line $[D_8]$ (414-term canonical representative) and $\ker[A]$ on $R_8^- = 0$, at the primes $2147483647$, $10^9+7$, $998244353$, $999999937$. The relaxation $\Psi(D_8;u_8) \notin J$ modulo $10^9+7$ and $998244353$ (and, in the referee replay, $999999937$); by the regular-sequence argument (the E1 system has the Koszul nullity 954 over $\mathbb Q$ and mod $p$, so a $p$-integral rational lift of $u_8$ exists) this excludes every certificate with $D = c D_8 + gF$ for the canonical representative, **without** an exact lift. It says nothing about $D_8 + w(F)$ with $\deg w_i = 3$ (141 unknowns, 468 quadrics), which is open.
3. **$d = 9$:** $\ker[A]$ is exactly 21-dimensional in each parity and lies in the $(01)$-invariant sector. The equivariant Hilbert series of $R$ under $\langle\tau\rangle\times\langle(01)\rangle$ gives sector dimensions $322 \to 301$ for $(\pm,+)$ and $301 \to 322$ for $(\pm,-)$ ($[A]\cdot$ maps $(\mathrm{par},\sigma)$ to $(1-\mathrm{par},-\sigma)$), forcing $\ge 21$ exactly; mod-$p$ kernels of $21$ (two primes) and $0$ in the anti-invariant sectors give equality. No sector is forced below degree 9; at $d = 10$ the forced kernels are $170+124 = 294$ (Y-even, equal to the computed value) and $152+109 = 261$ (Y-odd, computed $262$).
4. **Sum principle.** For every structured non-Jacobian factor tested (49 of them: $p_2X, p_2Y, p_{XY}, e_2X, e_2Y, X_0^2 \pm Y_0^2, X_0X_1 \pm Y_0Y_1, (X_0-X_1)^2 \pm (Y_0-Y_1)^2, X_0Y_1 \pm X_1Y_0, X_2^2, X_0X_1, X_2Y_2$, the cubics $p_3X, e_3X, \mathrm{Vdm}_X, \mathrm{Vdm}_Y, s_{XY^2}, s_{X^2Y}, \sum X_iY_jY_k, \mathrm{Hesse}, p_3Y, e_3Y$, the quartics $p_2X^2, p_2Xp_2Y, p_{XY}^2, p_4X, s_{X^2Y^2}, p_4Y, X_0^4 - X_0^2Y_0^2 + Y_0^4, X_0^4 - X_1^2Y_0^2$, and the sextics $\mathrm{Vdm}_X^2, \mathrm{Vdm}_Y^2, \mathrm{Vdm}_X\mathrm{Vdm}_Y, \prod((X_i-X_j)^2 \pm (Y_i-Y_j)^2), p_6X, p_6Y, \sum T_i, B_2, A, p_{XY}^3, O_1, O_2, O_3, F - \mathrm{Fermat}$), one has $\mathrm{Ann}_6(D) := \{A' \in R_6^- : A'D \in J\} = 0$ (mod $10^9+7$; an upper bound, hence rigorous). Since classes supported on $V(D_1D_2)$ are sums of classes supported on $V(D_1)$ and $V(D_2)$ (prescribed-support theorem of the project's Chapter 1 plus the weight argument), and $\mathrm{served}(D)/J_6 \subseteq \mathrm{Ann}_6(D)$, no product of these factors, in any degree, serves $A$ or any other nonzero class. The same holds for the Hessian forms of the partials, the $X$-Euler field, pair-radial, $\mathrm{std}$-radial, swap, rotation and cyclic-shift fields.

**Screened only (modular, no transfer):** $A D_8 \in J$ exactly over $\mathbb Q$ (true modulo six primes; the exact lift of $u_8$ failed for height reasons); the reduced fixed-$D$ system of $D_8$ is inconsistent at three primes but its rank could not be certified (0 of 159 null vectors lifted). An earlier internal status line "no certificate with $\deg D \le 7$" was not a theorem at the time; it is now a theorem for $\deg D \le 6$.

**Open:** degree 7 (Y-even relaxation built: 60 unknowns / 480 quadrics; msolve was killed after about two hours; odd and mixed sectors not started); the degree-8 family $D_8 + w(F)$; everything from degree 9 on, where E1 alone no longer excludes. None of this is a nonexistence claim.
