## Dense rational — wave-2 exclusion update (referee-checked), 1 November 2026

Prepared for `kejace` with Claude (Anthropic) assistance; no human review yet; nothing Lean-verified; no certificate claimed.

This continues `fqlx`'s 20 October packet (same set-up: $E_1$: $N(F)=AD$, $E_2$: $D\,\mathrm{div}N-N(D)=FC$ after Euler normalization, $J=(F_0,\dots,F_5)$, $(J:A)_d=\{D: AD\in J\}$). Everything below was replayed by a second, independently written set of scripts at a prime not used before ($p=1000000009$) or exactly over $\mathbb{Q}$; mod-$p$ results are transferred to $\mathbb{C}$ only where the rank caps that justify the transfer were checked (details in the linked referee report).

**Proved (all parities, arbitrary quotient, complex coefficients).**

1. $(J:A)_d=J_d$ for $d\le 9$ in both $Y$-parities. Hence every $E_1$-admissible denominator of degree $\le 9$ is a polar divisor $D=w(F)=\sum_i w_iF_i$ with $\deg w_i=d-5$; non-polar admissible denominators start at degree $10$, where the kernels of $[A]$ on $R_{10}^{\pm}$ are $294$ and $261$ (counting bound attained at two primes; not re-run here). Re-verified here for $d\le 8$ in both parities at $p=1000000009$ with both rank caps explicit (previously: $p=2147483587$ for $d\le 6$; even sector through $d=9$ at $2147483647$; both parities for $d=7,8,9$ at $1000000007$; $d=9$ at $101$ and $10007$ in the dossier).
2. Degree $5$: for every direction $v\in\mathbb{C}^6$ (not only the six coordinate ones), $D=v\cdot\nabla F$ fails $E_2$: $E_2$ forces $v^{T}\mathrm{Hess}(F)\,v\in (J:A)_4=0$, and the map $\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathbb{C}^6\to S_4$, $S\mapsto\sum S_{ij}F_{ij}$, has exact rational rank $21$.
3. Degree $6$, independent re-proof of `fqlx`'s $\deg D\ge 7$: for $D=w(F)$ with $w$ linear, $E_2$ implies $w^{T}\mathrm{Hess}(F)\,w\in J_6$. After removing the Euler gauge this is a homogeneous quadratic system (even sector $218$ quadrics in $17$ unknowns, odd $218/18$, mixed $426/35$). msolve `-g 1` returns DRL leading ideals containing a pure power of every variable (at $p=1073741789$ and again at $p=1000000009$ with an independent generator), so the only solution over $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$ is $0$; a homogeneous system over $\mathbb{Q}$ with a nonzero complex solution has a nonzero $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$-solution for every prime at which it is integral, so there is none over $\mathbb{C}$. **Every legal denominator has degree $\ge 7$, mixed parity included.**
4. Degree $7$, structured mixed-parity polar families. Same reduction with $w$ quadratic and quotient $J_8$ (both parities; $951$ quadrics), restricted to a linear family of fields and gauge-fixed. m-primary leading ideals, hence no legal $D=w(F)+\ell F$ with $w$ in the family, for: $w_i=z_i\,g_i$ with $g_i$ linear ($30$ unknowns); $w_X\in\mathbb{C}[X]_2$, $w_Y\in\mathbb{C}[Y]_2$ ($36$); $w_X\in\mathbb{C}[Y]_2$, $w_Y\in\mathbb{C}[X]_2$ ($36$); $w=\nabla g$, $g$ cubic ($56$). Each certified at two primes.

**Not proved / open.** The full degree-$7$ systems are not decided: the two $\tau$-pure sectors ($483$ quadrics in $60$ unknowns each) were run with msolve `-g 1`, $32$ threads; the even run was killed inside the F4 degree-$4$ step (about $410{,}000$ pairs selected) and the odd run was still in that step (matrix $\approx 870{,}767\times 500{,}646$, $\approx 39$ GB resident) after $2.5$ h. So **no statement about $\tau$-pure or $\tau$-stable degree-$7$ denominators is made**, and nothing is claimed at degree $7$ outside the four families above, at degree $\ge 8$, or about served-target spaces (the dossier's "rigorous over $\mathbb{C}$" served-target statement for $F_{6,0}$, $F_{0,6}$ is modular evidence only: projections of solution spaces need not reduce injectively). A generic degree-$7$ or degree-$8$ polar field is beyond the Gröbner range used here; the mixed degree-$7$ system has $951$ quadrics in $120$ unknowns.

**Bookkeeping fixes to earlier drafts.** The wave-1 statement that the degree-$7$ $\tau$-pure exclusion was rigorous is withdrawn (those msolve runs never finished). "A legal degree-$7$ $D$ is squarefree" should read: by the prescribed-support theorem (book, Ch. 1) one may take $D$ squarefree, and then $\tau$-stable support is equivalent to $\tau$-pure $D$. "Not F4-decidable" is a Fröberg-count heuristic, not a theorem. The mod-$p\to\mathbb{C}$ transfer for the $E_1$ sweep also needs the generator matrix of $J_{d+6}$ to keep its rank mod $p$; this holds in every logged line.
