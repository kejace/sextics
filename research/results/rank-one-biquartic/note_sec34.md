
## 4. Mathematical ideas developed (new in this attack)

### 4.1 The deformation is invisible modulo L  (proved, elementary)
`F_i = f_i + 2t L L_i M` and `F_{3+j} = g_j + t L² M_j` all lie in `J(F₀) + (L)`, and conversely `f_i, g_j ∈ J(F) + (L)`. Hence

**Proposition.** `J(F) + (L) = J(F₀) + (L)`, so `R/LR ≅ R₀/LR₀` where `R = S/J(F)` and `R₀ = S/J(F₀) = R_f ⊗ R_g` (the split Jacobian ring, `F₀ = f + g`).
By Macaulay duality in the Gorenstein ring `R` (socle degree 24), `dim Ann_R(L)_k = dim (R/LR)_{24−k} = dim (R₀/LR₀)_{24−k}`, which is **independent of t** and computable from the ternary ring `R_f` alone:
`dim Ann_R(L)_k = Σ_m dim Ann_{R_f}(L)_m · dim (R_g)_{k−m}` (with `Ann_{R_f}(L)_m ≅ coker(L : (R_f)_{10−m} → (R_f)_{12−m})^∨`; socle degree of `R_f` is 12, Hilbert function 1,3,6,10,15,18,19,18,15,10,6,3,1). For `k = 7` this gives `≥ 8·1 + 4·3 = 20` (generic value 20).
The same statement is **false for M** (`F_{3+j} = g_j + tL²M_j ∉ J(F₀) + (M)`): this is the algebraic source of the X/Y asymmetry of the annihilator (`Ann(X0³)_7 = 3`, `Ann(Y0³)_7 = 0`) — the coupling is quadratic in L but linear in M.

### 4.2 Structure of the E1-kernel (annihilator of A in R), degrees 7-9
Let `K_d = {D ∈ S_d : A D ∈ J}/J_d` (Y-even unless stated). Computed facts (mod p = 2^31−1, second prime 2^31−19 where stated):
* `K_d = 0` for `d ≤ 6` (both parities; proved over C by the rank argument of the dossier §4.2: the modular ranks of the J-blocks equal the regular-sequence values).
* `K_7 = 3` (Y-even), `K_7^{odd} = 0`; `K_8 = 9` (both parities); `K_9 ≥ 27` (Ann(X0³)_9 = 27 both parities).
* **`K_7 = Ann(X0³)_7 ⊂ Ann(L)_7`** — every multiplier in `{L, X0³, X0·L, L², LM, f, g, X0³Y0, X0³Y0²}` kills all three directions; `X0², X0²X1, X0²Y0, X0²Y0², X0²Y0³` kill exactly one; `X0, X1, X0X1, X0X1X2, Y0, Y0², Y0³, M, M², X0Y0, X0M, X0Y0³` kill none (kmult.py). In particular `A·K_7 ⊂ J` is explained by `X0³·K_7 ⊂ J`, not by any Y-content: the annihilator "sees" only the X-block and the coupling form L.
* **`K_7` is T-base-type and of the special shape `D = P₇(X) + L(X)·M(Y)·β₃(X)`** (block localisation inside the 73-dim space `span{P(X) M^a g^b}`: the kernel already lies in `S₇ ⊕ M·S₅`, and the finer localisation `S₇ ⊕ LM·S₃` is reported in §2.6). Geometrically `V(D) ∩ X = {L/M = −L²β₃/P₇ =: σ(X)}`: the relative scale `s = L/M` of the two-pencil model is tied to the X-point only; on `V(D)` the Y-pencil parameter `k = g/M³ = −u(X)σ³ − tσ²` is a rational function of `X`, so `V(D)/τ = ⋃_{X∈ℙ²} C'_{κ(X)}` is a family of Y-pencil curves over `ℙ²_X` — exactly the "compact-Y / base-type" class of supports that the packet's written obstruction excludes, and the fixed-D tests agree (§2).
* **Generation:** `K_8^{odd} = K_7 · ⟨Y0,Y1,Y2⟩` (all 9 directions), but `K_8^{even} ⊋ K_7 · ⟨X0,X1,X2⟩` : the products give only 6 of the 9 directions, so **three new annihilator generators appear in degree 8**; they are again Y-shape base-type (`P(X) M^a g^b`), and no X-shape (`Q(Y) L^a f^b`) direction occurs in any degree ≤ 8 (kbase.py).
* Consequently every E1-admissible denominator of degree ≤ 8 is `(base-type) + (polar dF(G))`, or `(base-type)·ℓ(Y)` for Y-odd degree 8. The first E1-admissible denominators that are *not* of this form are the degree-9 sums `Σ_i K_i(X,M)·H_i(Y)` with `H_i` Y-quadrics not proportional to `M` (admissible because `Ann` is an ideal); their divisors `{Σ (P₇,i + LMβ_i) H_i(Y) = 0}` correlate `X`, the scale `s`, and the Y-coordinates — the first family outside the packet's obstruction. They are untested at the full-system level (cost: cleared system 59332 × 12089 per member; see §5).

### 4.3 Why the t = 0 denominator cannot be continued, in one line
With `D = f`, `N = A·(X0,X1,X2,0,0,0)/6` (the strict t = 0 certificate) the defect on `F = F₀ + tL²M` is exactly `E = −(2t/3)·A·f·L²M` (direct computation: `ΣN_i ∂_i(tL²M) = (2t/3)AL²M`, the two `tL²M`-terms of the correction cancel). Since `A f L²M ∉ F²S + Im T_f` (D = f is inconsistent even at rank level, §2), and since the whole degree-6 E1-admissible family is `dF(G)` with `G` block-linear (18 parameters, all sampled members inconsistent), any certificate has a denominator that does not specialise to `f`.

### 4.4 What a support would have to look like
In the two-pencil model `X/τ = {(X,Y,s) : u(X)s³ + ts² + k(Y) = 0}` (`u = f/L³`, `k = g/M³`, `s = L/M`), the target `A = x0³y0³` is the class `ω_u ⊗ ω'_k ⊗ (2-form on the (u,s)-base)` with `ω_u = x0³ Ω_X/d(f−uL³)` and `ω'_k = y0³ Ω_Y/d(g−kM³)` the holomorphic forms of the two genus-10 pencil curves. Base-type divisors `{s = σ(X)}` only carry 2-forms of type `(1-form on ℙ²_X) ∧ ω'_k`, whose Gysin images are Lefschetz-trivial (target_image of f, g, R_X, L, M, hyperplanes all serve exactly `J₆`, §2.4) — in agreement with the packet. A working divisor must restrict `ω_u` non-trivially along the X-curves, i.e. it must be a correspondence between the pencils that is *not* a graph over `ℙ²_X` or over `ℙ²_Y` (sister condition for X-shape, which is empty anyway by 4.1). The smallest algebraically admissible such divisors are the degree-9 mixed sums of 4.2; the natural geometric candidates are fibre products over curves `B ⊂ (u,k)`-plane that are not graphs (e.g. `{u = φ(k)}` of degree ≥ 2 in both variables) — their defining polynomials `g^a M^{3b} f^c L^{3e}`-combinations have degree ≥ 12 (`g p³ + tL²M p q² + f q³` type in the note's §1.3), beyond reach of the dense solver on this machine today.
