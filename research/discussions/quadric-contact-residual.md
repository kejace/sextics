### 1. Universal Koszul–$J_{2d}$ Reduction and Complete Exclusion of $\deg D \le 6$ over $\mathbb{C}$

For every such $K$, mixed second partials of $F$ cancel identically in $\operatorname{div}(K) = (\partial_r Q_{rs})\partial_s F - (\partial_s Q_{rs})\partial_r F \in J_d$, and every component $K_\ell \in J_{d+1}$. Since $6F = \sum_i Z_i \partial_i F \in J_6$, **every Koszul syzygy $K \in \ker(\cdot\nabla F)_{d+1}$ and every $F Q$ term drops out identically modulo $J_{2d}$**:
$$B_D(N_0 + K) - F Q \equiv B_D(N_0) \pmod{J_{2d}} \qquad \forall D \in S_d,\; K \in \ker(\cdot\nabla F)_{d+1},\; Q \in S_{2d-6}.$$
In particular, whenever $D = \sum_{i=0}^5 C_i \partial_i F \in J_d$ (with canonical E1 lift $N_0 = A C$), the full E2 equation reduces modulo $J_{2d}$ to the pure symmetric Hessian congruence:

### 2. Degree $d = 7$ Character-Sector Exclusions and the Degree $d = 8$ Generator $D_8^*$

The polynomial $F$ is invariant under a $48$-element affine diagonal subgroup $G_{\mathrm{aff}} \subset \operatorname{GL}_6(\mathbb{C})$ generated by:

- $\sigma_2: (X_2, Y_2) \mapsto (-X_2, -Y_2)$,
- $h_1: (X_1, Y_1) \mapsto (\zeta_6 X_1, \zeta_6^{-1} Y_1)$ ($\zeta_6 = e^{2\pi i/6}$),
- $\tau_Y: (Y_0, Y_1, Y_2) \mapsto (-Y_0, -Y_1, -Y_2)$.

Every monomial $Z^e = X_0^{e_0} X_1^{e_1} X_2^{e_2} Y_0^{e_3} Y_1^{e_4} Y_2^{e_5}$ transforms by the character
$$\chi(e) = \bigl(e_0 + e_3 \bmod 2,\; e_2 + e_5 \bmod 2,\; e_1 - e_4 \bmod 6,\; e_3 + e_4 + e_5 \bmod 2\bigr) \in (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^2 \times \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z},$$
with $\chi(F) = (0,0,0,0)$ and $\chi(A) = (0,0,0,1)$.

#### Degree $d = 7$:

  $$\dim(J_F : A)_7 = 126 = \dim J_7.$$
  That is, $(J_F : A)_7 = J_7$ has **zero non-Jacobian E1 directions**.
- **Complete E2 exclusion of all $8$ $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^3$ parity sectors (and all $48$ pure character sectors):**

  - The $2$ parity sectors with $(b_0, b_2) = (1, 1)$ ($y \in \{0, 1\}$, $12 + 12 = 24$ parameters) are eliminated completely ($24 \to 0$) by linearly independent square columns modulo $J_{14}$ even when both $y$-parities are combined.

- At $(t_1, t_2) = (2, 3)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, the weighted Jacobian ring $R(F_{\mathrm{sub}}) = \mathbb{Q}[X_0, Y_0, U, V] / (\partial_{X_0} F_{\mathrm{sub}}, \partial_{Y_0} F_{\mathrm{sub}}, \partial_U F_{\mathrm{sub}}, \partial_V F_{\mathrm{sub}})$ has the exact K3 Hilbert series

def ref_fraction_rows(rows):
    pivots = {}
    for row in rows:
        vec = {k: v for k, v in row.items() if v}
        while vec:
            p = min(vec.keys())
