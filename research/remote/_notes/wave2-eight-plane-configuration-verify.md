## General logic points (apply to several rows)

1. **Direction of modular arguments.** Lower bounds on ranks transfer from F_p to Q; kernel dimensions transfer from Q to F_p. A *projected* kernel {D : AD ∈ J} or a *consistency* verdict needs the additional "no rank drop of the Jacobian part" (resp. of the system matrix). For E1 this is checked (rows 13–15). For fixed-D "inconsistent" verdicts it was **not** checked in wave 1; the wave-2 exact-Q Koszul-reduced ranks are the right repair (rows 16, 18).
2. **Parity WLOG vs. degree bounds** (row 12). The reduction D ↦ D·τD proves that *some* certificate has an even denominator, not that a minimal-degree one does. Degree bounds for d ≤ 9 reduce to D = w(F) with w of **any** parity; sweeps of J_d must include mixed w. Row 19 covers mixed w at d = 5; row 20 (d = 6 mixed) is pending.
3. **Sum principle / "inconsistent mod p".** No sum-principle application appears in this lane (q = 1 only). All "inconsistent" statements in wave 1 are single-prime, compressed-row modular computations; they are evidence unless backed by an exact-Q or two-prime+no-drop argument.
4. **What is rigorous right now (my assessment):** deg D ≥ 10, or D = w(F) with 6 ≤ d ≤ 9 and A·Hess_F(w,w) ∈ J_{2d}; at d = 6 only mixed-parity w remains (pending row 20). All of degree ≤ 5 is excluded over C.
