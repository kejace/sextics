## 1. Exact target

### 1.1 Statement

Variables `Z = (X0, X1, X2, Y0, Y1, Y2)`, q = 1,

```
F = X0^6 + X1^6 + X2^6 + Y0^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6
    + (1/2) X0^3 X1 Y0 Y1 + (1/2) X1^3 X2 Y1 Y2 + (1/3) X0^2 X1 X2 Y0 Y2
A = X0^3 Y0^3
```

Monomial support of F: six pure sixth powers and three mixed monomials with exponent vectors (3,1,0,1,1,0), (0,3,1,0,1,1), (2,1,1,1,0,1). Every mixed term has X-degree 4 and Y-degree 2; F has no (2,4) or (6,0)/(0,6)-mixed part. F(X,-Y) = F, A(X,-Y) = -A (Y-degree of A is 3).

Certificate degrees (q = 1): deg D = d (any d >= 1), deg N_i = d + 1, deg Q = 2d - 6, the cleared identity
`A D^2 - D Σ N_i ∂_i F + F (D Σ ∂_i N_i - Σ N_i ∂_i D) = F^2 Q` lives in degree 2d + 6. Euler normalization (Chapter 1) makes `N(F) = A D` exact and reduces the problem to `B_D(N) := D div N - N(D) ≡ 0 mod F`. The normalized form implies the necessary **colon condition** `A·D ∈ J(F) = (∂_0F, …, ∂_5F)`, i.e. `D ∈ (J(F) : A)`; it is vacuous for d >= 19 (Jacobian ring socle degree 24).

The formal contract imposes no parity on D (D may be Y-odd); at q = 1 the prescribed-support theorem (Chapter 1) makes existence depend only on the reduced support `V(D)_red`: existence over D ⇔ existence over any D' with the same reduced support.

### 1.3 Symmetry group

[NEW, PROVEN by enumeration] Diagonal substitutions `Z_i ↦ ζ^{w_i} Z_i` preserving F: 216 including scalars; projectively `G ≅ Z/6 × Z/6` of order 36, generated by

### 1.5 Ages of the diagonal elements (Kollár–Larsen test) [NEW]

For `γ ∈ G` and an eigenspace S (set of coordinates sharing a weight, |S| >= 2), the fixed locus `X ∩ P(S)` is nonempty and the tangent weights of γ on X there are the relative weights of the coordinates outside S (the normal direction has the eigenvalue of S because dF is homogeneous of degree 5 and F is invariant). ## 6. Feasibility: explicit witness vs. nonconstructive argument; what Lean would require

* **Explicit witness.** The protected statement is `HodgeComputer.HasCertificate F A 1`; an explicit `(D, N, Q)` is checked by kernel evaluation of the cleared identity plus homogeneity and complex coprimality (the latter is the only non-mechanical part; for D with rational coefficients one must prove coprimality over C, e.g. via a specialization/Eisenstein argument or by exhibiting `D` nonzero on a point of F = 0, as in the one-reflection and d48 submissions). Accepted witnesses go up to degree 60 (one-reflection, strict) and 48 (two-ternary-dense). For off-paired every witness has degree >= 7 and, by Section 3, almost certainly >> 8; a witness of degree 20–60 in the symmetric sector would have 10^4–10^6 coefficients but is still within what the verifier has accepted. Finding it is the problem: no structured family (products of H, C, Δ, mirrors, Jacobian factors) has worked so far, and the one explicit construction technique for this family (hidden reflection + Hessian) is unavailable. Probability of an explicit witness from E1–E4 alone: ~15 %.
* **Nonconstructive (geometric) existence.** A proof that `H^4(X)^G ⊂ N^1` would settle existence mathematically (prescribed-support theorem + §3.6), but none of the book's mechanisms applies (no age < 1 element, no reflection serving A's sector, no paired-sign surface, no cover with an exchange); and even if found, it is not Lean-feasible: the geometric inputs (mixed Hodge modules, Kollár–Larsen, Gysin/Leray) are far outside Mathlib. The holdout precedent shows the viable pattern: a geometric idea (cover + quotient with covering rational curves) is *replaced* by a finite-algebraic construction (polynomial curve family with nonsingular incidence, polynomial normal, integration, trace, transport) that Lean can check with the shared library (`PolynomialCurves`, `HoldoutCover`, `ResidueTransport`, `HigherResidues`, `EllipticCompanions`, `TransitiveCubic`, `AlgebraicSpecialization`). So the realistic Lean route is: find an auxiliary variety on which the class is served by an *all-polynomial-source primitive theorem* (E6/E7 style), with explicit source transport identities back to `(F, 1, X0^3Y0^3)`, then formalize (previous such proofs: 59 Lean files for the holdout, ~60 for the cubic routes). Estimated effort after the mathematics exists: weeks.
* **What any Lean proof must supply** (either route): the exact protected `(F, q, A)`; a nonzero homogeneous D with `IsCoprime`-type legality over C; six homogeneous numerators of degree `deg D + 1`; the identity `E = F^2 Q` (Q may be nonzero); only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. The existential route additionally needs irreducibility of F (Eisenstein specialization, 1.6), the nonvanishing of every chart denominator used, finiteness of the field extensions (transcendence-degree argument), and the homogeneous extraction of Chapter 1 / `HomogeneousCertificate`.
* **Honest assessment.** The instance was built to defeat the known serving mechanisms and three strong agent programs (≈ 1000 files of exact computation) have confirmed that the obvious reductions are obstructed while finding no certificate. The most informative next steps are E5 (which decides a whole class of routes), E4 (cheap, never run, complete for existence up to degree), and E6 (the only kind of idea that has ever solved a member of this family).
