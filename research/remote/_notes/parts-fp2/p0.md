## 0. Executive summary

* F = H(X0,X1,Y0,Y1) + B(X2,Y2): a coupled four-variable core (two binary sextics f(X0,X1), g(Y0,Y1) tied by `(5/7)X0^3X1Y0Y1 + (10/7)X0X1Y0^3Y1`) plus a separately even binary sextic B(X2,Y2). A = X0^3Y0^3 lives in the core and is even in X2 and Y2.
* Every published mechanism is excluded by the record and the book: the two coordinate reflections (X2, Y2) serve only targets odd in them; the 3+3 split theorem needs two ternary Fano companions, whereas the 4+2 cyclic covers are a Calabi–Yau threefold T_H: u^3=H ⊂ P(1,1,1,1,2) (K=0) and a genus-4 curve C_B: w^3=B ⊂ P(1,1,2) whose companion W_B is K3; the binary-block theorem needs three decoupled binary blocks; subset-Euler needs deg_I A + |I| = 6 (fails: 6+4, 0+2); the Hessian symmetrizer space is 2-dimensional so no other linear split exists.

At p=13 it does split as (13T^2−2T+1)·(quartic) — an ordinary supersingular/CM coincidence at one prime, not a global splitting.
