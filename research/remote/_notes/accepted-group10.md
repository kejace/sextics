## 1. twoties-tie2-x2-quartic-strict (PR #12, discussion #74)

**Data.** `F = X0^6 + X1^6 + Y0^6 + X0^2 X1^2 Y0^2 + X2^6 + Y1^6 + Y2^6 + X2^2 Y1^2 Y2^2` (two disjoint

**Method.** "Contact conic in the square-coordinate plane", transported to the second block; explicit strict
quartic witness. Constructive, d = 4, Q = 0.

**Witness (PROVEN).** `D = X2^4 - Y1^2 Y2^2 = (X2^2 - Y1Y2)(X2^2 + Y1Y2)` (reducible over Q: two quadric
hypersurfaces; D is torus-invariant). `N2 = Y1 (X2^2 Y1^2 - Y2^4)/6`, `N4 = -X2^3 Y2^2/3`, `N5 = X2 Y1^3 Y2/3`,
degree 5; `div N = N(D)/D = 2 X2 Y1^3/3`.

**Mechanism (VERIFIED).** In `(s,t,r) = (X2^2, Y1^2, Y2^2)` the block is the cubic `Q = s^3+t^3+r^3+str` and
`D = s^2 - t r` is a conic. On the parametrization `(s,t,r) = (σρ, σ^2, ρ^2)` the cubic restricts to
`σ^6 + 2σ^3ρ^3 + ρ^6 = (σ^3+ρ^3)^2 = (σ+ρ)^2(σ^2-σρ+ρ^2)^2`: the cubic curve meets the conic in three
double points (perfect-power contact, m = 2 in the sense of Chapter 2.3; the root `σ^3+ρ^3` is **not** a
polynomial in `s,t,r`, so this is the rational version of the contact identity). Pulling the conic back to P^5
gives D. The first tie is passive: N has no X0, X1, Y0 component, so `N(F) = N(F_2)` and the first block
does not enter E1 at all; E2 does not involve F. The witness is **literally the accepted
`f3-block-x0-quartic-strict` witness under the relabeling `(X0, X1, Y0) -> (X2, Y2, Y1)`** (A, D, N0->N2,
N1->N5, N3->N4 all match exactly). The coefficient -5/3 is forced: at this D the sector's strict served space is
`J ⊕ <X2^3Y1^3 - (5/3) X2Y1Y2^4>` with `J = <Y1 dF/dX2, X2 dF/dY1> = <3X2^5Y1 + X2Y1^3Y2^2, 3X2Y1^5 + X2^3Y1Y2^2>`.

## 2. twoties-crossblock-hesse-cofactor-d4-rational (PR #20, discussion #82)

**Data.** Same F as §1; q = 1; `A = X2^3 Y0 Y1 Y2` (four odd coordinates; the factor Y0 comes from the first

**Method.** Hesse cofactor quartic in the second block times a **passive multiplier**. Constructive, d = 4, Q = 0.

## 8. Relation to open instances
There are 16 open instances (`off-paired, dense-rational, cycle-q2, polarized-k3-remainder, klein-coupled-character,
symmetric-sextic-residual, rank-one-biquartic, four-plus-two-residual, quadric-contact-residual, one-reflection-skew,

the X/Y-type sextics have empty `sameVarietyInstances`). In particular the named pairs cycle-three/cycle-q2,
cyclic-triple-fano/-residual, reflection-coupled-fano/-residual, quadric-contact-broken/-residual,
one-reflection/-skew, holdout/off-paired, fermat-split/rank-one-biquartic, dense-rational all live in the

(HoldoutCover, EllipticCompanions, ResidueTransport, TransitiveCubic), not the explicit-witness pattern here.
VERIFIED structural contrast: **no open instance is coordinatewise even** (every open F has a monomial with an
odd exponent), so the (Z/2)^6 sector decomposition, the passive-multiplier lemma with a coordinate multiplier,
and the Hesse/cyclotomic cofactor-in-squares denominators do not transfer directly. Torus torsion groups of the
open F (SNF): off-paired `(Z/6)^2`; cycle-q2 `Z/2 x (Z/6)^2`; four-plus-two-residual, quadric-contact-residual,
one-reflection-skew `(Z/2)^2 x Z/6`; cyclic-triple-residual `Z/3 x Z/6`; reflection-coupled-residual `(Z/2)^2`;
klein-coupled-character `Z/14`; all dense/polarized ones `Z/2` only.
The only open instance with a binary block in squares is `four-plus-two-residual`
(`X2^6 + (15/7)X2^4Y2^2 + (10/7)X2^2Y2^4 + Y2^6` in `(X2^2,Y2^2)`), but its target `X0^3 Y0^3` lies in the
coupled quaternary block `(X0,X1,Y0,Y1)`; the only conceivable transfer is using the binary block as a spectator
"channel" in a denominator (speculative, untested).
Transferable general lessons: (a) for a fixed D the problem is small exact linear algebra once symmetry sectors
are used (complete by Chapter 1); (b) `(D, BN)` is strict for `B A0` whenever `(D,N)` is strict for `A0` and
`N(B) = 0`; (c) D need not be a symmetry eigenvector; (d) denominators that omit one variable get coprimality
for free in Lean via `relPrime_of_specialization` + `relPrime_of_retract`.

## 9. Reconstructed families, predictions, open observations
### 9.1 Y-type companion family (RECONSTRUCTION; VERIFIED on 3 fibers incl. one prediction)
For `p = u^3 + μu^2w + νuw^2 + γw^3`, put `M = u + βw` and `q = p - M^3 = w·[(μ-3β)u^2 + (ν-3β^2)uw + (γ-β^3)w^2]`.
If `q = w·ℓ^2` is a perfect square times w, i.e. β is a root of
`Φ(β) = -3β^4 + 4μβ^3 - 6νβ^2 + 12γβ + ν^2 - 4μγ = 0`
(231: `Φ = -(β-1)(3β^3-5β^2+13β+1)`, β = 1; fiberA: `Φ = -(β+2)(3β^3+2β^2-4β-4)`, β = -2; each has a unique
rational root), then `D_β = M^2 - M Y1^2 + Y1^4` serves `J ⊕` two new Y-type directions including
`X0Y0^2Y1(X0^2 + c Y0^2)`, with numerator `N_act ∝ Y1[D_β d/dX0 + (M - Y1^2) W_ℓ]`, `W_ℓ = -(aX0^2+3bY0^2)d/dX0 + 2aX0Y0 d/dY0`
for `ℓ = au + bw`, plus a forced Y1-component. Prediction tested on the new fiber `(μ,ν,γ) = (0,3,1)`
(`Φ = -3(β-1)(β^3+β^2+7β+3)`): served dim 4 at β = 1 (vs 2 at the control β = 2) and explicit strict witness
with `c = 3`. Rationality of a root β is the only obstruction to a rational quartic D of this type; a general
one-block sextic needs a degree-4 algebraic β (then trace/norm would give a rational denominator of degree 16).
Not derived: closed form of the Y1-component and of `c` beyond the line `(μ,3,1)` where `c = 9/(3+2μ)`.

### 9.2 X-type pencil family (OBSERVATION, partly unexplained)
Spectator coefficients in D equal nonzero roots of `disc_u((u+w)^3 - s p)`; non-root coefficients yield only
Jacobian targets in all tests. The role of the channel multiplicity (why exactly three channels at two distinct
roots serve the monomial `X1Y0^5` on the 113 fiber, and why one root-channel serves two bundles) is open.
Also untested: whether a cube `(u + βw)^3` with β ≠ 1 gives further rational denominators.

### 9.3 Scope caveats
All "forced coefficient" statements are relative to the **fixed** record denominators (and their sign sector);
they say nothing about other denominators (Chapter 1: fixed-D failure never excludes support elsewhere), and the
all-target theorems of Chapters 2/2a guarantee that every monomial on these sextics has some certificate.
